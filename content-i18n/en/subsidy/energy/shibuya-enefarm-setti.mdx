Shibuya Ward's ENE-FARM installation subsidy covers part of the installation cost for home fuel cell systems (ENE-FARM), aimed at cutting CO2 emissions and promoting energy generation within the ward.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The subsidy is a **flat ¥300,000**
- Applications are accepted **May 1, 2026 – March 15, 2027**, and pre-approval is required
- Even within the application window, the program **ends once the budget runs out**

## 1. Who qualifies

You must meet all five of the following requirements:

- The applicant (the buyer) is an individual registered as a resident of Shibuya Ward, or planning to become one by the time the installation completion report is filed
- You're installing a new, unused ENE-FARM unit at a home in the ward that you live in
- The equipment is designated as a home fuel cell system by the Fuel Cell Promotion Association
- You haven't already received a subsidy under this program in the same fiscal year
- No outstanding resident tax delinquency

## 2. Amount

A flat ¥300,000.

## 3. How to apply

Applications are accepted from May 1, 2026 through March 15, 2027. You must apply for pre-approval first, and submit a completion report to the ward by March 15, 2027. Regardless of the application window, the program ends once the budget runs out.
