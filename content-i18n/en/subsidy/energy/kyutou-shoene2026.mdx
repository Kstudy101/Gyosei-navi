The Efficient Water Heater Program 2026 is a Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry subsidy for installing high-efficiency water heaters — EcoCute heat pumps, hybrid water heaters, or home fuel cells (ENE-FARM). It's funded through a ¥57 billion supplementary budget for fiscal year 2025, aimed at improving energy efficiency in water heating, which accounts for a large share of household energy use.

## Key points

- The base subsidy runs **¥70,000 to ¥170,000 per unit**, depending on the equipment
- Additional amounts are available if performance requirements are met, and also for removing an old unit
- Homeowners don't apply directly — a **registered Efficient Water Heater Business** handles the paperwork

## 1. Who qualifies

The subsidy covers installing a high-efficiency water heater in either a detached house or a multi-unit residence, as follows:

| Application category | Housing type | Eligible applicant |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase/construction | Newly built custom home | The home's builder |
| Purchase/construction | Newly built subdivision home | The home's buyer |
| Purchase/construction | Existing home (renovation) | The person commissioning the work |
| Purchase/construction | Existing home (purchase) | The home's buyer |
| Lease | Newly built custom home, newly built subdivision home, or existing home (renovation) | The water heater's lessee |

Projects that already received a subsidy under the Efficient Water Heater Program 2025 don't qualify, nor does "owner-supplied equipment," where the person commissioning the work buys the water heater themselves and only asks the contractor to install it. Work must have started on or after November 28, 2025.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

The subsidy is the sum of ① the base amount, ② a performance add-on, and ③ a removal add-on (if ② and ③ don't apply, you just get ①).

| Water heater installed | Base amount | Performance add-on | Base amount cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heat pump water heater (EcoCute) | ¥70,000/unit | ¥30,000/unit | Up to 2 units for detached houses, 1 for multi-unit residences |
| Hybrid water heater | ¥100,000/unit | ¥20,000/unit | — |
| Home fuel cell (ENE-FARM) | ¥170,000/unit | No add-on | — |

If you remove an old unit alongside installing a high-efficiency water heater, you get an additional removal add-on (¥40,000 per unit, up to 2 units, for removing an electric storage heater; ¥20,000 per unit for removing an electric water heater). This removal add-on runs on a target budget of ¥3.6 billion and will end once that budget is used up.

## 3. How to apply

Homeowners can't apply directly. A pre-registered "Efficient Water Heater Business" — a contractor, retailer, or leasing company — handles the application, then passes the subsidy on to the homeowner.
