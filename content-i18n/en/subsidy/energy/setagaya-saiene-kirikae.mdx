Setagaya Ward's renewable energy switch subsidy encourages residents to switch their electricity plan to a 100% renewable (or effectively 100% renewable) plan. **Unlike the ward's equipment-installation subsidies, this isn't a program residents apply for directly at the ward office.**

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- When a resident switches to an eligible electricity plan, **the electric company pays back up to ¥15,000**
- **You apply through the electric company, not the ward office** (a ward-approved "Renewable Energy Switch Promotion Partner" runs the campaign)
- The first campaign round for fiscal 2026 runs **June 1 – September 30, 2026**, and may end early depending on budget usage

## 1. How the program works (and how it differs from other wards' equipment subsidies)

In Chiyoda, Minato, and Shibuya wards, subsidies work by having residents or businesses buy and install equipment, then apply directly to the ward for reimbursement. Setagaya Ward's renewable energy switch subsidy works indirectly instead:

1. A retail electricity provider registers with the ward as a "Renewable Energy Switch Promotion Partner" under Setagaya's decarbonization initiative, the "UCHIKARA Project"
2. During a period the ward designates, the partner company runs a campaign limited to Setagaya Ward residents
3. Based on the number of new contracts (households) from the campaign, **the ward pays a subsidy to the partner company**
4. The partner company pays back the resident who switched their contract

The ward's official page explicitly states that applications aren't accepted online, in person, by mail, phone, fax, or email. To use this program, residents need to sign up through the campaign run by the electric company (not the ward office).

## 2. Who qualifies and how much you get back

This is for Setagaya Ward residents who switch from a standard (non-renewable) electricity plan to a 100% renewable or effectively 100% renewable plan. You get back up to ¥15,000 per new contract (household) on an eligible plan.

The following companies are running the first campaign round for fiscal 2026 (June 1 – September 30, 2026):

<Checklist items={["UPDATER Inc.", "Seikatsu Club Energy Co., Ltd.", "JCOM Marketing Co., Ltd.", "TG Octopus Energy Co., Ltd.", "Tokyu Power Supply Inc."]} />

Since campaign conditions and payback methods vary by company, check the details on the UCHIKARA Project's dedicated page.

## 3. Budget progress

According to the ward's official page, as of September 11, 2026, the first campaign round's budget was 42.1% used. The ward has stated the campaign may end earlier than its scheduled date (September 30, 2026) depending on how the budget is used up.
