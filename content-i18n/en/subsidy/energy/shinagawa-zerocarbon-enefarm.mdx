Shinagawa Ward's ENE-FARM subsidy is one menu under the ward's "Zero Carbon Action Subsidy," covering part of the cost when a ward resident installs a home fuel cell system (ENE-FARM).

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The ENE-FARM subsidy is **up to ¥200,000**, against a planned quota of **30 units** (still showing "room available" as of September 15, 2026)
- The application period runs **May 25, 2026 – March 15, 2027**, first-come, first-served until the quota is reached
- **Several other categories in the same program — solar power, storage batteries, EcoCute, ZEH, and more — have already stopped accepting applications** as of September 15, 2026, after exceeding their planned quota

## 1. What Shinagawa's Zero Carbon Action Subsidy covers

Shinagawa Ward's "Zero Carbon Action Subsidy" is a ward-run program bundling together many energy-saving and energy-generating categories — solar power, storage batteries, ENE-FARM, EcoCute, ZEH, EVs, and more. Each category has its own eligible applicants, subsidy amount, and planned quota, and application status varies by category.

## 2. Who qualifies for the ENE-FARM subsidy

- Applicants must be Shinagawa Ward residents
- Eligible costs cover the equipment unit, accessories, and the full installation cost
- The equipment must be installed between April 1, 2026 and March 15, 2027

## 3. Amount and application status

| Eligible equipment | Eligible applicant | Subsidy | Planned quota | Application status (as of September 15, 2026) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ENE-FARM | Residents | Up to ¥200,000 | 30 units | Room available |

For reference, here's the application status (as of September 15, 2026) for other major categories in the same program:

| Eligible equipment | Application status |
|---|---|
| Solar Power System A (residents, small/medium businesses, etc.) | Quota exceeded |
| Storage Battery System A (residents, small/medium businesses, etc.) | Quota exceeded |
| EcoCute and hybrid water heaters | Quota exceeded |
| ZEH and Tokyo Zero Emission Housing | Quota exceeded |
| ZEV (electric vehicles, etc.) | Accepting applications |

All applications the ward receives by 5:15 PM on the day the planned quota is reached are treated as simultaneous, and a lottery is held. If budget becomes available again — due to a finalized award amount coming in lower than expected, or an application being withdrawn — the ward may reopen applications (any reopening will be announced in the ward newsletter or on the official site).
