Chiyoda Ward's EV charging equipment subsidy covers part of the cost when someone in the ward installs clean-energy vehicle charging equipment. Fast chargers, standard chargers, and V2H (Vehicle-to-Home) equipment all qualify.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- Fast chargers get up to **¥500,000**, standard chargers up to **¥300,000**, and V2H equipment up to **¥500,000**
- Even if you apply for multiple units, the **combined cap is ¥500,000 (pre-tax)**
- You can **combine this with Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry or Tokyo metropolitan subsidies** (apply to the ward after the national/metropolitan amount is finalized)

## 1. Who qualifies

You qualify if you fall into one of these categories:

- Owner or resident of a new or existing home in the ward (including those with the owner's consent)
- Owner or lessee of a new or existing business premises in the ward
- Manager or management association of a new or existing condominium in the ward

Government offices don't qualify. Common requirements include using equipment designated as eligible under the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry's subsidy program, the equipment being unused and newly installed, not being a lease purchase, and having made a "Chiyo-Eco Declaration."

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

| Category | Subsidy (per unit) |
|---|---|
| Fast charger | Up to ¥500,000 |
| Standard charger (including charging outlets and outlet stands) | Up to ¥300,000 |
| V2H | Up to ¥500,000 |

Even if you apply for multiple units, the combined subsidy cap is ¥500,000 (pre-tax).

## 3. How to apply

If you're combining this with national or metropolitan subsidies, you apply to the ward after those amounts are finalized.

<Checklist items={["If using national or Tokyo metropolitan subsidies, complete those procedures first", "Prepare the full subsidy application package (check the pamphlet for details)", "Submit by mail or in person at the Environmental Policy Division counter on the 5th floor of the ward office, by the application deadline (February 26, 2027) (in-person visits require an appointment)", "After receiving the review results notice, submit the subsidy payment request form"]} />

Applications are accepted on a first-come, first-served basis, and the subsidy ends once the budget runs out.
