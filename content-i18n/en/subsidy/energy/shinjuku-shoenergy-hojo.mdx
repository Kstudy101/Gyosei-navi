Shinjuku Ward's subsidy for energy-saving and energy-generating equipment covers six types of equipment in one package: solar power systems, eco-friendly heat-pump water heaters, home fuel cells (Ene-Farm), high-reflectivity roof coatings, insulated windows, and battery storage systems. Unlike most programs in other wards, it's an **"after-the-fact" application system** — you apply only after construction and full payment are complete.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key takeaways

- A **fan-shaped subsidy program** covering six types of equipment, each with its own subsidy amount
- Battery storage systems get **¥10,000 per kWh of storage capacity, capped at ¥100,000** (conditional on being permanently connected to solar power or a home fuel cell)
- Rather than applying in advance, you **apply after construction and payment are finished**. There are four application rounds a year, and each round closes early once its budget runs out

## 1. Who's eligible

Eligible applicants are people registered as residents of Shinjuku Ward who install eligible equipment for personal use at their own individual residence and have completed payment in full. Residents of multi-unit buildings are eligible only if they installed insulated windows.

Battery storage systems must meet both of the following conditions:

- Designated as eligible equipment by the Sustainable Open Innovation Initiative (SII), or recognized as equivalent
- Permanently connected to a solar power system or a home fuel cell (Ene-Farm)

You're not eligible if you've already received this subsidy for the same category of equipment before, if you installed used or leased equipment, or if more than a year passed between the completion of installation and the completion of payment.

## 2. Subsidy amounts and how they're calculated (six types, for individual residences)

| Eligible equipment | Subsidy amount |
|---|---|
| Solar power system | ¥100,000 per kW of rated output (capped at ¥300,000) |
| CO2 refrigerant heat-pump water heater (Eco Cute) | Flat ¥100,000 |
| Home fuel cell (Ene-Farm) | Flat ¥100,000 |
| High-reflectivity coating (roof) | ¥2,000 per m² installed (capped at ¥200,000) |
| Insulated windows | 25% of installation cost before tax (capped at ¥100,000) |
| Battery storage system | ¥10,000 per kWh of storage capacity (capped at ¥100,000) |

## 3. Application period and status

FY2026 applications are split into four rounds. The eligible period for equipment completion is the same across all rounds: April 1, 2026 to March 12, 2027.

| Round | Application period | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Round 1 | May 25–July 31, 2026 (must arrive by deadline) | Closed |
| Round 2 | August 17–October 16, 2026 (must arrive by deadline) | Open |
| Round 3 | November 2–December 25, 2026 (must arrive by deadline) | — |
| Round 4 | January 12–March 12, 2027 (must arrive by deadline) | — |

As of September 14, 2026, the projected approval rate for Round 2 is 24% (76% of the budget remaining). Even within a round's stated period, applications close as soon as that round's budget is used up.

## 4. How to apply
