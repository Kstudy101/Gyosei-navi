Minato Ward's ENE-FARM subsidy covers part of the equipment cost when a ward resident newly installs a home fuel cell system (ENE-FARM). It's one of the menus under the ward's own "Global Warming Countermeasures Subsidy Program."

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The subsidy is **equipment cost ÷ 4, capped at ¥150,000** (installation cost isn't included)
- It's for **individual residents only** (management associations, small and medium-sized enterprises, and sole proprietors can't apply)
- You must apply **before construction begins** — applications after work has started don't qualify

## 1. Who qualifies

You must meet all of the following:

- Apply before construction begins (if applying for a newly built home, apply before the ENE-FARM is installed)
- Use new equipment (used or leased equipment doesn't qualify)
- The equipment must have a rated output of 0.3kW to 1.5kW, a hot water tank of at least 20 liters, and overall efficiency of 80% or higher
- No prior ENE-FARM subsidy application at the same address
- Be a Minato Ward resident living at the installation address at the time of application

If you live outside the ward and build a home in it to install the equipment, you can only apply after transferring your residency and becoming a Minato Ward resident.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

| Applicant | Calculation | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Residents | Equipment cost ÷ 4 | ¥150,000 |

Equipment cost includes the fuel cell unit, hot water storage unit, and accessories, but not installation labor.
