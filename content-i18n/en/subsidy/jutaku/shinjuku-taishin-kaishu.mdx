Shinjuku Ward's wooden home seismic retrofit subsidy (officially, "Subsidy for Seismic Retrofit Work on Wooden Buildings") covers wooden houses built to the old or new seismic standards, with the ward subsidizing part of the cost of seismic retrofit work. It's offered as one menu item under the ward's Building Seismic Resilience Support Program.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key takeaways

- Seismic retrofit work that raises the structural score to 1.0 or above is subsidized at **three-quarters of the eligible cost, capped at ¥3 million**
- Simplified retrofit work raising the score to between 0.7 and just under 1.0 is subsidized at **three-fifths, capped at ¥1.5 million**
- Buildings that **jut into the road or lack road access** get a lower subsidy rate (capped at ¥1.5 million or ¥750,000 instead)

## 1. Who's eligible

Eligible buildings are wooden houses of two stories or fewer, or combined shop-and-residence buildings where more than half the total floor area is residential, meeting one of these two conditions:

- **Old seismic standard**: construction started on or before May 31, 1981
- **New seismic standard**: construction started between June 1, 1981 and May 31, 2000, using conventional post-and-beam construction

Applicants must be the owner, or a family member or resident with the owner's consent. For jointly-owned (condominium-style) buildings, either a resolution from the management association's general meeting or consent from a majority of co-owners is required. Individual applicants must not be behind on resident tax; corporate applicants must qualify as a small or medium-sized business under the Small and Medium-sized Enterprise Basic Act. Applicants also must not have received, or currently be receiving, a correction order from the ward for an illegal building.

## 2. How much you get and how it's calculated

The eligible construction cost is whichever is lower: the actual cost of the work, or an amount calculated as total floor area × ¥39,900/m².

| Type of work | Subsidy rate | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Seismic retrofit work (structural score 1.0 or above) | 3/4 of eligible cost | ¥3 million |
| Simplified retrofit work (structural score 0.7 to under 1.0) | 3/5 of eligible cost | ¥1.5 million |

If the building **juts into the road** (extends past the road boundary line) or has **no road access** (the lot doesn't have at least 2 meters of frontage on a road), the following lower rates apply once additional conditions are met:

| Type of work (jutting into road / no road access) | Subsidy rate | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Seismic retrofit work | 3/8 of eligible cost | ¥1.5 million |
| Simplified seismic retrofit work | 3/10 of eligible cost | ¥750,000 |

For detached houses occupied by people with disabilities and similar residents, the rate is more generous: the full eligible cost for seismic retrofit work (capped at ¥3 million), or half for simplified retrofit work (capped at ¥1.5 million). Consumption tax isn't covered by the subsidy, and amounts are rounded down to the nearest ¥1,000. Projects already receiving another subsidy aren't eligible.

## 3. How to apply and the schedule

You must sign your construction contract only after the subsidy has been officially approved. The subsidy is available only within the ward's budget for the fiscal year.

For the FY2026 cycle, the completion report for the wooden-home seismic retrofit subsidy must be submitted by the end of November 2026. Since the ward doesn't publish exact application opening or closing dates, it's recommended you consult the seismic team early if the schedule is tight.
