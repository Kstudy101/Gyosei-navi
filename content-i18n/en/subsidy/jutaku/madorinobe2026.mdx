The Advanced Window Renovation 2026 Program is a Ministry of the Environment subsidy for upgrading the windows and doors of existing residential and non-residential buildings to higher-insulation products. It's funded through a ¥112.5 billion supplementary budget for fiscal year 2025, aimed at decarbonizing homes and cutting utility costs.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The cap is **¥1,000,000 per housing unit** (non-residential buildings range from ¥1,000,000 to ¥10,000,000 depending on size)
- Homeowners don't apply directly — a **registered contractor (Window Renovation Business)** handles the paperwork
- The program closes once the budget is used up, so **acting early matters**

## 1. Who qualifies

The subsidy covers work that improves the insulation performance of openings (windows and doors) in existing detached houses, multi-unit residences, and non-residential buildings. The person commissioning the work (typically the homeowner) is the one who benefits, but openings that already received a subsidy under the earlier "Advanced Window Renovation Program," "Advanced Window Renovation 2024 Program," or "Advanced Window Renovation 2025 Program" don't qualify again.

Work must have started on or after November 28, 2025.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

The subsidy is the sum of fixed amounts per product, based on the performance and size of the product installed and the type of building it's installed in. Only products confirmed to meet the program's performance requirements are eligible.

| Building type | Maximum subsidy |
|---|---|
| Residential | ¥1,000,000 per unit |
| Non-residential (total floor area ≤240m²) | ¥1,000,000 per building |
| Non-residential (total floor area >240m²) | ¥10,000,000 per building |

If the total subsidy for an application comes to less than ¥50,000, it isn't eligible.

Eligible work includes glass replacement, interior window installation, exterior window replacement (cover method or chipping method), and door replacement (cover method or chipping method). Specific subsidy amounts by product are listed on the relevant work pages of the official site.

## 3. How to apply

Homeowners can't apply directly. A "Window Renovation Business" (contractor or similar) registered with the program office handles the application, then passes the subsidy on to the homeowner.
