Shibuya Ward's subsidy for seismic retrofit and demolition costs for wooden homes covers part of the cost of retrofit or demolition work on wooden homes that the ward's seismic diagnosis found have a structural score below 1.0.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- You need to start with a **free seismic diagnosis**, and it must find a structural score below 1.0
- The retrofit subsidy caps out at **¥1,500,000** for elderly households
- The application deadline is generally **December 25, 2026** (the program may close earlier if the budget runs out)

## 1. Get a seismic diagnosis first

Shibuya Ward's seismic diagnosis is entirely free, including the survey by a specialist consultant (a first-class registered architect), the diagnostic report, and a retrofit proposal. It covers detached houses, row houses, and multi-unit residences in the ward that started construction on or before May 31, 1981, or two-story-or-lower conventional post-and-beam buildings that started construction between June 1, 1981 and May 31, 2000.

The results are given as a "structural score." A score below 1.0 qualifies you for the retrofit or demolition subsidy.

## 2. Who qualifies

To receive the retrofit or demolition subsidy, you must meet all of the following:

- The ward's seismic diagnosis found your building's structural score below 1.0
- The building owner is a registered resident of Shibuya Ward
- The applicant is the one commissioning the work
- The building generally complies with the Building Standards Act and related regulations (any non-compliant parts must be corrected alongside the seismic retrofit)
- In principle, the work must be completed by the end of January of the application fiscal year

The construction contract must be signed after the ward approves the subsidy — contracts signed before approval don't qualify.

## 3. Amount and how it's calculated

The figures below are based on Shibuya Ward's published fiscal 2025 pamphlet. We recommend checking with the ward for the latest amounts.

| Renovation type | Household type | Subsidy | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| General renovation (complies with Building Standards Act, etc.) | Non-elderly | Half the construction cost | ¥1,000,000 |
| General renovation (complies with Building Standards Act, etc.) | Elderly | Full amount up to ¥500,000, plus 2/3 of anything beyond that | ¥1,500,000 |
| General renovation (doesn't fall under the above) | Non-elderly | Half the construction cost | ¥560,000 |
| General renovation (doesn't fall under the above) | Elderly | Full amount up to ¥500,000, plus 2/3 of anything beyond that | ¥1,060,000 |
| Demolition (built on or before May 31, 1981) | — | Half the construction cost | ¥1,000,000 |

"Elderly" refers to people 65 or older, or people who would need assistance evacuating in a disaster.

## 4. How to apply

<Checklist items={["Apply for the ward's seismic diagnosis consultant dispatch program (attach a certified copy of the registration and a property tax notice)", "Receive the seismic diagnosis report", "If the structural score is below 1.0, sign a design and construction supervision contract with the ward's seismic diagnosis consultant", "Apply for subsidy-eligibility approval and receive the ward's project approval notice", "After approval, sign a construction contract with your contractor (contracts signed before approval don't qualify)", "After the work is complete, apply for subsidy payment with your completion notice and receipts attached"]} />

The application deadline is generally December 25, 2026, but the program closes once it hits its set budget, so it's recommended you check on the application status before proceeding.
