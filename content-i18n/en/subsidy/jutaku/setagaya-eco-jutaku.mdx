Setagaya Ward's Eco Housing Subsidy covers work that improves a home's energy efficiency, including window insulation renovation and high-insulation door installation. It subsidizes up to ¥200,000 combined across four types of eligible work.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- Eligible work covers four types: **window insulation renovation, high-insulation door installation, high-insulation bathtub installation, and high-reflectivity roof renovation**
- The cap is **¥200,000 combined** across all four categories
- Applications run in two rounds, and the **second round is scheduled to open October 1, 2026** (as of September 20, 2026, applications are in a gap between rounds)

## 1. Who qualifies

You qualify if you meet all of the following:

- You're an individual registered as a ward resident who contracts and pays for the renovation
- The building being renovated is in the ward
- The contractor doing the work is based in the ward
- You're doing one of the four eligible types of work (window insulation renovation, high-insulation door installation, high-insulation bathtub installation, or high-reflectivity roof renovation)

Companies and newly built homes don't qualify. As of fiscal 2026, insulation material installation, solar power systems, solar thermal systems and water heaters, and exterior wall painting — all previously eligible — no longer qualify.

## 2. Amount

Up to ¥200,000 combined across the four categories. The subsidy for high-reflectivity roof renovation (currently ¥70,000) is under consideration for an increase to ¥100,000 starting with the second-round applications, but this hasn't been finalized as of September 20, 2026.

## 3. How to apply and when

Applications run in two rounds with a pre-registration requirement. After signing a construction contract, you register your email address, complete pre-registration (which secures your place in the budget), have the work done, and then submit your application electronically once the work is finished.

<Checklist items={["Consult and contract with a contractor (must be based in the ward)", "Register your email address after signing the construction contract", "Complete pre-registration (required to secure your planned subsidy amount)", "Complete the work and payment", "Gather the required documents and submit your application electronically"]} />

The pre-registration window for the first round has already closed (it ended July 21, 2026). Pre-registration and applications for the second round are scheduled to open October 1, 2026, covering work completed between September 1, 2026 and January 31, 2027. If you pre-registered in the first round but your work was delayed, you can still apply in the second round as long as you meet its requirements — but your first-round budget allocation expires on August 31, so you'll need to pre-register again starting October 1.
