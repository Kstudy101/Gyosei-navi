Shinagawa Ward's Home Improvement Subsidy Program (Eco & Barrier-Free Home Renovation) covers part of the construction cost when residents, condominium management associations, and others use a ward-based contractor to do renovations focused on environmental or accessibility improvements.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- Residents get 10% of the (pre-tax) construction cost, **capped at ¥200,000**
- Condominium management associations and individual owners of rental housing get a cap of **¥1,000,000**
- The **income requirement was removed** starting in fiscal 2026, and you can now combine this with national or Tokyo metropolitan subsidy programs

## 1. Who qualifies

To apply as a resident, you must meet the following:

- Be a resident of Shinagawa Ward (if you haven't transferred your residency by the time you apply, you may not be able to receive the subsidy)
- Currently live in the home being renovated (which must be in the ward), or plan to live there after renovation
- Have no outstanding resident tax delinquency
- Have the owner's consent if you're renting

Common requirements also include: this being your first time using the program, contracting with a ward-based contractor, and the total eligible (pre-tax) construction cost being at least ¥100,000.

As of fiscal 2026, the program changed slightly: the application requirement shifted from "head of household" to "resident (anyone with an address there)," and the income requirement was removed.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

| Applicant | Subsidy rate | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Residents | 10% of the (pre-tax) construction cost | ¥200,000 |
| Condominium management associations, individual owners of rental housing | 10% of the (pre-tax) construction cost | ¥1,000,000 |

## 3. Eligible work

Eligible work includes the following types of renovation to existing homes:

<Checklist items={["Eco home renovation: LED lighting fixture installation, heat-reflective coating, insulation, high-insulation bathtub installation, water-saving toilet installation, new ventilation equipment, and more", "Barrier-free home renovation: handrail installation, removing level differences, widening hallways or entrances, door renovation, bathroom renovation, toilet renovation, and more", "Other: lightening the roof, installing fire-resistant exterior wall panels, installing crime-prevention glass or doors, work that improves seismic resistance, and more"]} />

## 4. How to apply

The application period runs from April 1 (Wednesday), 2026 through January 29 (Friday), 2027. Documents must be submitted both before and after the work, and you must submit the subsidy application form within one month of completing the work. Both work completion and the subsidy application submission must happen by February 26 (Friday), 2027. Applications close once the subsidy budget is used up.
