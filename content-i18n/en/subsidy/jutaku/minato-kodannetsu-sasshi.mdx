Minato Ward's high-insulation sash subsidy covers part of the cost when existing homes are renovated with high-insulation sashes (windows). Individual residents can receive up to ¥100,000, and condominium management associations can receive up to ¥10,000,000.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- For residents, the subsidy is installation cost ÷ 4, **capped at ¥100,000**
- For management associations, the cap is **¥10,000,000** (but every window in every unit must be renovated)
- You must apply **before construction begins** — newly built homes don't qualify

## 1. Who qualifies

Common requirements include applying before construction begins, using new equipment registered with the Hokkaido Environmental Foundation, and not having previously applied for this subsidy at the same address.

To apply as a resident, you must be registered as a Minato Ward resident at the time of application and live at the installation address. If you live outside the ward but buy a condo in the ward to install interior windows, or if you live in the ward but install windows at a property outside it, you don't qualify.

To apply as a management association, you must be a management association within the ward (rental properties don't qualify), and you must renovate every window in every unit.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

| Applicant | Calculation | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Residents | Installation cost ÷ 4 | ¥100,000 |
| Management associations, etc. | The lower of (installation cost ÷ 4) or (units renovated × ¥100,000) | ¥10,000,000 |

Screen doors aren't included in eligible costs.

## 3. How to apply and required documents

Application deadlines differ between residents and management associations.

| Applicant | Application deadline | Completion report deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Residents | January 29, 2027 (Friday) | February 26, 2027 (Friday) |
| Management associations, etc. | **September 30, 2026 (Wednesday)** | February 26, 2027 (Friday) |

<Checklist items={["Prepare the application form", "Prepare a copy of a valid quote (must clearly show the registered product model, manufacturer name, and itemized installation cost)", "Print out the results of the product eligibility search", "Take photos of the current condition before renovation (showing the full window frame)", "Submit before construction begins — by mail, in person, or through the electronic application form"]} />

Management associations also need additional documents, such as a certified copy of the building's registration (obtained within the past 3 months) and minutes from a management association general meeting.
