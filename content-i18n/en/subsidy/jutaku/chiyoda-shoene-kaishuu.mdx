Chiyoda Ward's energy-saving renovation subsidy covers part of the cost when homes, condominium common areas, or office buildings are upgraded with energy-saving equipment. Eligible work includes LED lighting, solar power, and window insulation.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- **The fiscal 2026 round hit its budget cap and applications are currently closed** (future plans are still being decided)
- Eligible work spans a wide range, from LED lighting to solar power to window insulation
- Subsidy rates run 20-50% of eligible costs depending on the work, with a cap of up to ¥1,000,000 for homes

## 1. Who qualifies

Eligible applicants are owners of existing buildings in the ward (including sectional ownership, or those with the owner's consent). All of the following conditions must be met:

- You haven't already received this subsidy for the same building in the same fiscal year (once per year)
- No outstanding property tax, resident tax, or other delinquencies
- The equipment being replaced is unused
- You've made a "Chiyo-Eco Declaration"

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

Subsidy rates and caps vary by target and category.

| Target | Category | Subsidy | Total cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homes | LED lighting | 50% of eligible costs | ¥1,000,000 |
| Homes | Solar power, storage batteries, ENE-FARM | 20% of eligible costs | ¥1,000,000 |
| Homes | Window insulation (interior window installation, exterior window replacement, glass replacement) | Combined rate by size and performance | ¥1,000,000 |
| Homes | High-efficiency gas water heater | ¥30,000 per unit | ¥1,000,000 |

Window insulation subsidy rates are broken down in detail by window grade (thermal transmittance) and size. The ward particularly recommends window insulation upgrades (double windows or multi-layer glass).

## 3. Current application status

The fiscal 2026 round received an overwhelming number of applications and has hit its budget cap, so applications are currently closed. The ward is still deciding how to handle the program going forward, and details will be posted on the official page once settled.
