Shinjuku Ward's Startup Funding loan is one of the ward's institutional loan programs for small and medium-sized businesses. The ward subsidizes most of the interest and part of the credit guarantee fee to arrange low-interest loans for people planning to start a business in the ward, or who started one less than 5 years ago. It's run under the official name "Startup Support Loan Program."

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key takeaways

- The loan limit is **¥20 million** (¥10 million for prospective founders, ¥15 million for corporate spin-offs)
- Of the 2.1%-or-under interest rate, the ward covers 1.9 percentage points or more, so **you pay under 0.2% yourself**
- The credit guarantee fee is also subsidized at **half of what you pay, capped at ¥260,000**

## 1. Who's eligible

Eligible applicants fall into one of these categories:

- People who are not currently business owners and plan to start a business as a corporation or sole proprietor
- People planning to start a business through a corporate spin-off
- People who started a business as a corporation or sole proprietor less than 5 years ago
- People who started a business through a spin-off less than 5 years ago

In every case, you must operate in an industry eligible for guarantee under the Tokyo Credit Guarantee Association, and must not be behind on resident tax or business tax. Corporations must have their head office (main place of business) and registered head office address both located within the ward; sole proprietors must have their place of business (main place of business) within the ward (if you've lived in the ward for at least a year, starting the business anywhere in Tokyo is also allowed).

Virtual offices used only for "registration" or "receiving mail," and offices under pay-per-use seating contracts, are not eligible (except for sole proprietors who have lived in the ward for at least a year).

## 2. How much you get and how it's calculated

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Loan limit | ¥20 million (¥10 million for prospective founders, ¥15 million for spin-offs) |
| Loan term | Up to 7 years (grace period up to 12 months) |
| Interest rate | 2.1% or under |
| Your share | Under 0.2% |
| Ward's share | 1.9% or more |
| Credit guarantee fee subsidy | Half of what you pay is subsidized (capped at ¥260,000) |

## 3. How to apply

Unlike a typical loan, Startup Funding requires multiple 2-hour interviews.

## 4. A related special program worth knowing about

If you rent a vacant shop (one that's been closed for at least a month) within a shopping association or shopping district promotion cooperative recognized by the ward and start your business there, you can use the "Shopping District Vacant Shop Utilization Support Fund." On top of Startup Funding's requirements, it adds conditions like joining the shopping association — but in exchange, it's a more generous program, covering **the full interest and credit guarantee fee**.
