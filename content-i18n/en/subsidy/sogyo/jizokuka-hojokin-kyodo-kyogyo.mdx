The Small Business Sustainability Subsidy (Joint/Collaborative Track) subsidizes part of the cost when a regional development organization supports five or more small businesses (participating businesses) working together to expand their market reach. Unlike the standard Sustainability Subsidy, the funding goes to the support organization rather than directly to the participating businesses.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- Only **regional development organizations** — chambers of commerce, societies of commerce and industry, and similar — can apply; individual small businesses can't apply directly
- The subsidy cap runs **¥20,000,000 to ¥30,000,000**, depending on the number of participating businesses
- The 3rd round closes at **5:00 PM on September 30, 2026**

## 1. Who qualifies

Eligible projects are ones where a regional development organization plays a leading role in providing one-stop support to five or more participating businesses — helping improve their products and services, build their branding, and access new sales opportunities. All three of the following must apply:

- The initiative is expected to have broad impact
- The initiative is sustainable
- The initiative provides one-stop support

Initiatives where participating businesses aren't directly involved (with the subsidized organization simply doing sales work on their behalf), or projects aimed at the applicant's own market expansion, don't qualify.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

| Number of participating businesses | Subsidy cap |
|---|---|
| 10 or more | ¥30,000,000 |
| 5-9 | ¥20,000,000 |

The subsidy rate is either a flat rate, or 2/3 of eligible costs.

## 3. How to apply

Applications go through jGrants (the electronic application system). Program guidelines, application forms, and FAQs are published on the official site.
