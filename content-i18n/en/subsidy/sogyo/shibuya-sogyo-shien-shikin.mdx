Shibuya Ward's Startup Support Fund Loan Brokerage is one of the ward's Small and Medium Enterprise Business Fund Loan Brokerage programs, arranging low-interest loans — with the ward covering nearly all the interest — for people planning to start a business in the ward or who started less than a year ago.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The loan amount is **up to ¥20,000,000** (¥4,000,000 for a personal vehicle)
- The rate is 1.7% a year or less, of which Shibuya Ward covers 1.6 percentage points, leaving **your cost at 0.1% or less**
- If you also meet the requirements for the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's "startup" loan program, **2/3 of your credit guarantee fee** is separately subsidized

## 1. Who qualifies

You qualify if you're a small/medium-sized enterprise (corporate or sole proprietor) under Article 2, Paragraph 1, Items 1, 2, or 5 of the Small and Medium-sized Enterprise Credit Insurance Act, and are either planning to start a business or started less than a year ago. Non-profit organizations (NPOs) don't qualify.

You must also meet the following:

- Be an individual not currently operating a business, with either "the knowledge and experience necessary for the business" or "a legally required qualification"
- Have a concrete business plan
- Be planning to start a business in the ward, or have started less than a year ago

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

| Loan name | Loan amount | Use of funds | Rate (fixed) | Loan period |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Startup Support Fund | Up to ¥20,000,000 (¥4,000,000 for a personal vehicle) | Working capital, equipment, or both at once | Your cost 0.1% or less (1.7% a year or less, of which Shibuya Ward covers 1.6%) | Within 7 years (including a 1-year grace period) |

If you meet the requirements for both Shibuya's "Startup Support Fund" and the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's "startup" loan program, Tokyo subsidizes 2/3 of your credit guarantee fee.

## 3. How to apply

The Startup Support Fund requires multiple meetings with a business consultant, and it takes about a month to get your brokerage letter issued.
