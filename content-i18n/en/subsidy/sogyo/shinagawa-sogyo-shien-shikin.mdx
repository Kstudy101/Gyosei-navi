Shinagawa Ward's Startup Support Fund Loan Brokerage is one of the ward's "Small and Medium Enterprise Business Fund Loan Brokerage" programs, arranging low-interest loans — with the ward covering most of the interest — for people and businesses starting up in the ward.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The brokerage limit is **¥20,000,000**
- For a "first-time startup," your cost is **0.2% or less** out of a nominal rate of 1.6% or less, and the **credit guarantee fee is fully subsidized**
- If you're certified under the Designated Startup Support Program, you get **zero interest through your third year**

## 1. Who qualifies

Applicants fall into four categories:

| Category | Definition |
|---|---|
| First-time startup | Someone who wasn't a company representative or sole proprietor starting a business in Shinagawa Ward for the first time, or within 5 years of a new startup or business succession |
| Second business | Someone already a company representative or sole proprietor starting a new business in Shinagawa Ward, within 5 years of continuing that business |
| Designated Startup Support Program special provision | Falls under "first-time startup" and has completed and been certified under Shinagawa Ward's Designated Startup Support Program |
| IT industry revitalization special provision | Falls under "first-time startup" and has a startup plan in the information and communications industry (Japan Standard Industrial Classification, mid-level category 39) |

Businesses that relocate outside the ward after starting up in Shinagawa, or where a change of representative comes with incorporation, don't qualify. Sole proprietors planning to start a business need self-funding equal to at least a third of their total required startup funds.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

| Applicant category | Brokerage limit | Your cost | Nominal rate [interest subsidy] | Guarantee fee subsidy rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First-time startup | ¥20,000,000 | 0.2% or less | 1.6% or less [1.4%] | Fully subsidized |
| Designated Startup Support Program special provision / IT industry revitalization special provision | ¥20,000,000 | Zero interest through year 3, 0.2% or less from year 4 | 1.6% or less [1.6% through year 3 / 1.4% from year 4] | Fully subsidized |
| Second business | ¥20,000,000 | 0.7% or less | 1.8% or less [1.1%] | Half subsidized |

The repayment period is within 10 years (including up to a 12-month grace period) for all categories.
