Setagaya Ward's Startup Support Fund Loan Brokerage is one of the ward's Small and Medium Enterprise Loan Brokerage programs, arranging low-interest loans — with the ward covering most of the interest — for people preparing to start a business in the ward or who started less than a year ago. The window is the Setagaya Industrial Promotion Public Corporation.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The loan limit is **¥20,000,000**
- The nominal rate is 2.0%, of which the ward covers 1.8%, leaving **your cost at just 0.2%**
- The window is the **Setagaya Industrial Promotion Public Corporation**, not the ward office (an appointment-based consultation is required)

## 1. Who qualifies

You qualify if you fall into one of the following:

**Before starting a business**

- If starting as a company, you'll set up your registered head office and main place of business in the ward from the outset
- If starting as a sole proprietor, you'll set up your main place of business in the ward from the outset

**After starting a business**

- Your registered head office and main place of business have been in the ward from the outset, and you're less than a year into your business as of the application date
- As a sole proprietor, your main place of business has been in the ward from the outset, and you're less than a year into your business

There are additional requirements, such as having no outstanding resident tax delinquency. Only the person starting the business can apply — representatives can't apply on their behalf.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

Conditions as of April 1, 2026 are as follows.

| Loan name | Use of funds | Limit | Nominal rate | Ward-covered rate | Your rate | Repayment period |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Startup Support Fund | Working capital / equipment | ¥20,000,000 | 2.0% | 1.8% | 0.2% | Within 7 years, including up to a 12-month grace period |

A credit guarantee is required, and Tokyo's guarantee fee subsidy program (2/3) may apply in some cases.

## 3. How to apply

<Checklist items={["Call the Setagaya Industrial Promotion Public Corporation to book a consultation (4th floor, Setagaya Sangyo Plaza)", "Consult with a startup advisor (a small/medium enterprise consultant), typically over about 4 sessions", "Prepare and submit a business plan using the ward's format, along with the Startup Support Fund Loan Brokerage Application, supported by relevant documentation", "If your application is approved, you'll receive a brokerage letter — take it to a participating financial institution for their review"]} />

Consultation and application time slots run Monday-Friday by appointment (60-minute slots, 5 per day).
