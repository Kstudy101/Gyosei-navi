Minato Ward's Startup and Startup Support Subsidy pairs help drafting a startup plan from a business consultant with cash subsidies covering part of the cost of starting a business, for businesses less than two years into their startup journey in the ward.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- Covers **2/3 of eligible (pre-tax) costs, up to ¥2,500,000** in cash (up to ¥1,600,000 in your first year)
- It's for businesses that **started up in Minato Ward and are less than two years in** at the time of application
- **About 100 slots** are available, and applications close once the budget is used up

## 1. Who qualifies

Businesses must meet all of the following:

<Checklist items={["Started up in Minato Ward, and less than two years into it at the time of application", "Have an office in Minato Ward (for companies, both the registered head office and main place of business must be in the ward; for sole proprietors, the main place of business must be in the ward)", "Completed a business consultation with Minato Ward's Industrial Promotion Division (advance reservation required) and prepared a startup plan", "Hold any required licenses or permits for their industry, or will obtain them before the subsidy is paid", "Qualify as a small/medium-sized enterprise under Article 2 of the Small and Medium-sized Enterprise Basic Act", "Don't fall under businesses regulated by the Act on Control and Improvement of Amusement Businesses, etc.", "Agree to a site visit for follow-up support and inspection by a specialist for three years after the subsidy is paid"]} />

Businesses registered only at a virtual office, deemed large enterprises, or already receiving similar subsidies — the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's Startup Subsidy, the startup track of the Small Business Sustainability Subsidy, or similar national, Tokyo, or other municipal subsidies — don't qualify, nor do businesses that previously received this same subsidy.

## 2. Amount and eligible costs

The subsidy rate is 2/3 of eligible (pre-tax) costs, capped at ¥2,500,000. In your first year, the combined cap across all expense categories is ¥1,600,000.

| Eligible cost | Cap |
|---|---|
| Rent (store/office rent; 3 months in year one, 9 months in year two) | Up to ¥1,200,000 (year one: ¥100,000/month × 3 months; year two: ¥100,000/month × 9 months) |
| Equipment costs (exterior/interior construction, equipment procurement and installation) | ¥600,000 |
| PR costs (flyer production, advertising placement, etc.) | ¥400,000 |
| Website creation costs (new creation only; revamping an existing site doesn't qualify) | ¥300,000 |

If you're using coworking space, the rent subsidy cap is ¥186,000 (year one: ¥15,600/month × 3 months; year two: ¥15,600/month × 9 months).

## 3. How to apply and the schedule

The application period runs from Wednesday, April 1, 2026 through Friday, January 15, 2027 (postmark valid). About 100 slots are available, and applications close once the budget is used up.
