Chiyoda Ward's Startup Loan Brokerage arranges low-interest loans, through a three-party cooperation between the ward, the Tokyo Credit Guarantee Association, and a designated financial institution, for people preparing to start a business or who recently started one — with the ward subsidizing part of the interest. You apply after working with a small and medium enterprise consultant on a business plan through a series of consultations in the ward.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The loan limit is **¥25,000,000 for residents, ¥15,000,000 for others**
- The nominal rate is 2.0% or less, of which 1.6% is subsidized, leaving **your cost at 0.4% or less** — a very low rate
- **Not subject to the responsibility-sharing system** (fully guaranteed), and available to those starting a business in the ward or who started less than a year ago

## 1. Who qualifies

Applicants must clearly be embarking on a business eligible for guarantee association coverage, and fall into one of the following:

**Before starting a business** (with a concrete plan to newly open as a sole proprietor within about a month, or newly incorporate within about two months)

- An individual not currently operating a business, who has self-funding equal to or greater than the loan amount plus the knowledge and skills needed for the business, and is preparing to start a business (the business location must be in the ward)
- A company that's a small/medium-sized enterprise continuing part of its existing business while newly establishing a company to start a new venture (must become the majority shareholder of the new company)

**After starting a business** (at the time of application, the company must be registered with its head office in the ward, or the sole proprietorship's main place of business must be in the ward)

- An individual who wasn't previously operating a business and started one less than a year ago
- A company that's a small/medium-sized enterprise that started a newly established company less than a year ago

Businesses not eligible for Tokyo Credit Guarantee Association coverage — financial services, adult entertainment businesses, religious corporations, and similar — don't qualify, nor do businesses operating only out of a virtual office with no verifiable physical presence.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

Conditions as of April 1, 2026 are as follows.

| Representative category | Loan limit | Nominal rate | Interest subsidy rate | Your cost | Repayment period |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Resident (representative lives in Chiyoda Ward) | Up to ¥25,000,000 | 2.0% or less | 1.6% | 0.4% or less | Within 7 years, including up to a 12-month grace period |
| Other | Up to ¥15,000,000 | 2.0% or less | 1.6% | 0.4% or less | Within 7 years, including up to a 12-month grace period |

The Startup Fund is exempt from the responsibility-sharing system, meaning the Credit Guarantee Association fully guarantees the loan thanks to the ward's guarantee fee subsidy. If you meet the requirements for the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's "startup loan" program, a separate Tokyo credit guarantee fee subsidy (2/3) may also apply.
