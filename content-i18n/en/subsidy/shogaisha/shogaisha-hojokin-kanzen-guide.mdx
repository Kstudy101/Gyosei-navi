Benefits for people with disabilities include the Assistive Device Cost Benefit (補装具費支給制度), which follows nationwide standards, and programs whose content varies greatly by municipality, such as the Daily Living Equipment Provision Program (日常生活用具給付等事業) and welfare taxi vouchers (福祉タクシー利用券). The most efficient approach is to understand the nationwide system first, and then check the programs offered by your own local government.

## Key Takeaways

- The **Assistive Device Cost Benefit** is nationwide. The user copayment is 10% in principle, with a monthly cap by income (¥37,200 per month for general households)
- The **Daily Living Equipment Provision Program** is mandatory for municipalities, but eligible items and amounts are "left to each municipality's judgment" — there is no nationwide amount
- Transportation support such as welfare taxi vouchers is an original program of each municipality. Amounts and eligibility differ greatly from one city or ward to another

## 1. Assistive Device Cost Benefit (補装具費支給制度) — Nationwide

Under the Act on Comprehensive Support for Persons with Disabilities, municipalities pay part of the cost of purchasing, repairing, or renting prosthetic limbs, orthoses, wheelchairs, hearing aids, canes, and similar devices. Municipalities run the program, but there is a nationwide framework in which the national government bears one half of the cost and the prefecture and the municipality each bear one quarter.

### Eligible Items (common to adults with physical disabilities and children with disabilities)

Prosthetic limbs, orthoses, postural support devices, safety canes for people with visual impairments, artificial eyes, eyeglasses, hearing aids, cochlear implants (repairs only), wheelchairs, electric wheelchairs, walkers, walking support canes (excluding T-shaped and straight canes), and communication devices for people with severe disabilities

### Eligible Items (additional items for children with disabilities only)

Seated posture support chairs, standing support devices, head support devices, and defecation aids

### User Copayment (monthly cap by income bracket)

| Income bracket | Monthly cap |
|---|---|
| Households receiving public assistance | ¥0 |
| Households exempt from municipal resident tax (low income) | ¥0 |
| Households subject to municipal resident tax (general) | ¥37,200 |

<Callout type="warning">
If the highest municipal resident tax income-based amount paid by the person with a disability or their spouse is ¥460,000 or more, the household is not eligible for the Assistive Device Cost Benefit.
</Callout>

There are two payment methods: "reimbursement" (償還払い), where you first pay the full amount and claim the benefit afterward, and "agency receipt" (代理受領), where the device supplier collects only your copayment and bills the remainder to the municipality.

Specific cap amounts for each item are set separately by notice of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare and are not covered in this article. Please confirm the actual amounts at the counter when you apply.

## 2. Daily Living Equipment Provision Program (日常生活用具給付等事業) — Differs by Municipality

Based on Article 77 of the Act on Support for Independence of Persons with Disabilities, this is one of the mandatory programs within the Community Life Support Program run by municipalities. The cost-sharing framework (national government one half, prefecture and municipality one quarter each) is the same as for the Assistive Device Cost Benefit, but **eligible items, benefit amounts and copayments are "left to each municipality's judgment,"** so there is no nationwide amount.

Eligible equipment falls into the following six categories.

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Nursing care and training support equipment | Special beds, special mats, etc. |
| Independent living support equipment | Bathing aids, indoor signal devices for people with hearing impairments, etc. |
| Home medical care support equipment | Electric suction devices, thermometers for the blind, etc. |
| Information and communication support equipment | Braille writers, artificial larynxes, etc. |
| Excretion management support equipment | Ostomy appliances and hygiene supplies, etc. |
| Home living movement aids | Equipment involving small-scale home modifications |

<Callout type="info">
This program is mandatory for municipalities, but the specific benefit amounts, eligible items and income limits differ greatly by local government. It is not a program that guarantees "this amount nationwide," so please check individually at the counter of the city, ward, town or village where you live.
</Callout>

## 3. Transportation Support (Welfare Taxi Vouchers, etc.)

Many cities and wards have set up their own subsidy programs, such as taxi vouchers, to support outings by people with disabilities. Amounts and eligibility requirements differ by local government. Examples from within Tokyo are explained in each municipality's article.
