Yokohama City's disability auto fuel voucher (障害者自動車燃料券) is a program that provides eligible residents with disabilities with fuel vouchers for a pre-registered private vehicle (excluding motorcycles). Rather than a direct cash payment, vouchers are used at contracted gas stations — a form of "in-kind benefit." The program covers similar disability categories as the city's [welfare taxi voucher](/subsidy/shogaisha/yokohama-fukushi-taxi-riyouken), but **you can only choose one** of the available transport support programs.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- Each voucher covers up to **¥1,000** in fuel; the standard issue is **24 vouchers per year** (those with kidney disease requiring hemodialysis 3+ times per week receive **48 vouchers per year**)
- You can only use **one of five programs**: this voucher, the welfare taxi voucher, welfare special passenger ticket, senior citizen special pass, or special passenger ticket
- Vouchers can only be used at **contracted gas stations** and only for the **one pre-registered vehicle**
- Continuing recipients receive next year's vouchers by mail and do not need to reapply each year

## 1. Eligibility

Applicants must live in Yokohama City, must not currently hold a welfare taxi voucher (福祉タクシー利用券), welfare special passenger ticket (福祉特別乗車券), senior citizen special pass (敬老特別乗車証), or special passenger ticket (特別乗車券), and must meet one of the following four conditions:

1. Hold a Physical Disability Certificate (身体障害者手帳) at grade 1 or 2, covering at least one of: lower limb, trunk, visual, internal organ, or mobility function disability
2. Hold an Intellectual Disability Certificate (愛の手帳 / 療育手帳) at grade A1 or A2, or have been assessed as having an IQ of 35 or below at a Disability Self-Reliance Consultation Office or Child Guidance Center
3. Hold a Physical Disability Certificate at grade 3 (covering at least one of the above disability types) AND hold an Intellectual Disability Certificate at grade B1, or have been assessed as having an IQ of 50 or below
4. Hold a Mental Disability Health and Welfare Certificate (精神障害者保健福祉手帳) at grade 1

<Callout type="info">
Even those holding a grade 1 or 2 Physical Disability Certificate are only eligible if the disability covers at least one of: lower limb, trunk, visual, internal organ, or mobility function. Not all grade 1 and 2 disability categories automatically qualify.
</Callout>

### Eligible vehicles

One vehicle per disabled person must be pre-registered and must meet the requirements below.

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Owner | The person themselves, their spouse, direct-line relatives and their spouses, siblings and their spouses, or a cohabiting relative. For installment purchases or long-term leases, the user listed on the vehicle inspection certificate must be one of the above. If none of the above own the vehicle, a person providing continuous daily caregiving to the eligible person may also register their vehicle |
| Vehicle type | Standard, compact, or light passenger vehicles with a seating capacity of 10 or fewer (or similar structure/function) |
| Ineligible | Commercial vehicles, corporate-owned vehicles (except installment/lease), paid mobility services, rental cars, taxis, light trucks, borrowed or substitute vehicles, etc. |

## 2. Amount and number of vouchers

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Subsidy cap | Up to **¥1,000** per voucher |
| Standard issue | **24 vouchers** per year (valid: October 1 – September 30 of following year) |
| Kidney disease (hemodialysis 3+ times/week) | **48 vouchers** per year (not applicable if dialysis facility provides transportation) |
| Eligible fuels | Premium gasoline, regular gasoline, and diesel only (oil, car wash, etc. not covered) |

Each voucher is used for refueling in ¥1,000 increments. The price per liter at the time of use may differ from the displayed price — confirm the rate before refueling.

## 3. How to apply

Apply at your **local ward Welfare Health Center (福祉保健センター)**.

<Checklist items={["Physical Disability Certificate (身体障害者手帳), Intellectual Disability Certificate (愛の手帳 / 療育手帳), or Mental Disability Health and Welfare Certificate (精神障害者保健福祉手帳)", "Copy of the vehicle inspection certificate (車検証) for the vehicle to be registered (for certificates issued after January 2023, also provide a copy of the 'Vehicle Inspection Certificate Record Details' sheet)"]} />

Continuing recipients will have next year's vouchers mailed to them automatically — no reapplication is needed. The notification letter refers to mailing by "late September" and "mid-September." If your disability certificate is not valid from October 1 onward, no vouchers will be issued — please renew your certificate early if needed.

For FY2026 renewals, a dedicated hotline (045-628-9350, August 21 to December 11, 2026) has been set up.

If you change your registered vehicle, bring the remaining vouchers, your certificate, and a copy of the new vehicle inspection certificate to your ward's Welfare Health Center. You will receive the same number of remaining vouchers, and no additional exchange will be possible afterward.

## 4. Relationship with other programs and switching

The following five programs are mutually exclusive — **you may only use one**.

1. Disability Auto Fuel Voucher (障害者自動車燃料券)
2. Welfare Taxi Voucher (福祉タクシー利用券) — [see separate article](/subsidy/shogaisha/yokohama-fukushi-taxi-riyouken)
3. Welfare Special Passenger Ticket (福祉特別乗車券)
4. Senior Citizen Special Pass (敬老特別乗車証)
5. Special Passenger Ticket (特別乗車券)

To switch to a welfare taxi voucher, welfare special passenger ticket, or senior citizen special pass during the validity period, **2 remaining fuel vouchers** (or **4** for those with a 48-voucher allocation) are counted as one month, which determines the start date of the new program. You cannot switch if you don't have enough remaining vouchers. Switching is allowed only **once per validity period**, and you must return the remaining fuel vouchers.

Switching to the welfare special passenger ticket requires an annual user fee of **¥1,200** (¥600 for those under 20). Switching to the welfare taxi voucher or senior citizen special pass has no user fee.

## 5. Important notes

- Lending, transferring, reselling, cashing out, or using as collateral is prohibited. Fraudulent use may result in recovery, suspension of issuance, and a repayment demand
- **Lost vouchers cannot be reissued**
- Vouchers can only be used at contracted gas stations — check the official Excel/PDF list (as of August 6, 2026) or the data map
- Contact (program inquiries): Health and Welfare Bureau, Disability Welfare and Health Division, Independence Support Section, Mobility Support Team (045-671-2401)
