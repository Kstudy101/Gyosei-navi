Shinjuku Ward's Prenatal and Postnatal Support Program isn't a cash payment like the "birth congratulation money" seen in some other wards — it **subsidizes the fee when you have a childcare/housework helper or postpartum doula visit your home**. Since you receive nothing unless you actually use a helper, this program is different in character from the birth-cost subsidies and congratulation payments offered by wards like Chiyoda, Minato, and Shibuya.

## Key takeaways

- **Not a cash payment — it subsidizes the fee for using a helper or postpartum doula** (postpartum doula ¥1,500/hour, helper ¥1,000/hour)
- Usable hours have a **cap based on the child's age bracket** (40 hours for a first-child-only household, up to 75 hours for multiples households)
- Requires **advance usage registration**; it takes about two weeks from registration to when you can start using the service

## 1. Who's eligible

You must be registered as a resident of Shinjuku Ward and fall into one of these categories:

- Pregnant
- Living with and raising a child under 1 year old
- Part of a multi-child household living with a child under 3 and a sibling under 4
- Living with multiples (twins, triplets, etc.) under 3 years old

## 2. Fees and usage limits

Fees are as follows (discounts and exemptions apply if the entire household is exempt from resident tax, or is on public assistance):

| Provider | Fee |
|---|---|
| Postpartum doula | ¥1,500/hour |
| Other providers (helpers) | ¥1,000/hour |

Usable hours vary in detail depending on the child's age bracket and household makeup.

| Child's age bracket | First child only (no siblings) | Multi-child household (has siblings) | Multiples household (twins) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prenatal to under 1 | 40 hours | 55 hours (sibling under 3) / 40 hours (sibling 3+) | 75 hours |
| 1 to under 2 | — | 20 hours (sibling under 4) | 60 hours |
| 2 to under 3 | — | 20 hours (sibling under 4) | 60 hours |

Postpartum doula services are only available from before birth through under 5 months postpartum. For triplets or more, an extra 30 hours per child is added. If you use support beyond your usage cap, you pay the provider's set fee entirely out of pocket.

## 3. How to apply

<Checklist items={["Submit a usage registration application at a counter (such as the Child Support Center), by mail, or online (a separate application is needed for each age bracket; applications open one month before the birthday that starts each bracket)", "Receive a usage registration decision notice from the ward (about two weeks after applying)", "Choose a provider from the list of contracted providers and contact them directly to book", "A helper or postpartum doula visits on your scheduled day and provides support", "Pay the usage fee using the method the provider specifies"]} />

You can use support once a day, choosing a 2, 3, or 4-hour session, starting at the top of the hour or at 15, 30, or 45 minutes past. Canceling after 5 p.m. on the business day before your scheduled use incurs a 1-hour cancellation fee.
