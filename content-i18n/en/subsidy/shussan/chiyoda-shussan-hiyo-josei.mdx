Chiyoda Ward's childbirth cost subsidy is a ward-run program that covers the gap when delivery costs exceed the national childbirth lump-sum allowance. It pays up to ¥310,000 per birth.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The subsidy covers **up to ¥310,000 per birth** (the cap stays at ¥310,000 even for twins or more)
- It applies to births from **April 1, 2025** onward
- The application deadline is **two years from the date of birth**

## 1. Who qualifies

You qualify if you gave birth on or after April 1, 2025, and meet all of the following:

- You had residency in the ward before the birth, and you've lived there continuously for at least one year as of the application date
- You're enrolled in domestic health insurance and received the childbirth lump-sum allowance
- The newborn has been registered at the applicant's address since the date of birth

Stillbirths or miscarriages at 85 days of pregnancy or later (including induced terminations) also qualify, as long as the residency and insurance conditions above are met. In principle, the person who gave birth applies. People receiving public assistance (生活保護) are not eligible.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

The subsidy is calculated as "actual delivery cost minus the childbirth lump-sum allowance = subsidy amount," capped at ¥310,000 per birth. If you also receive supplementary benefits or a refund through the high-cost medical expense system, those amounts are subtracted from the actual cost first.

| Example | Delivery cost | Lump-sum allowance, etc. | Subsidy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single birth | ¥700,000 | ¥500,000 | ¥200,000 |
| Single birth | ¥900,000 | ¥500,000 | ¥310,000 (cap) |
| Twins | ¥1,400,000 | ¥1,000,000 | ¥310,000 (cap) |

## 3. How to apply

The application deadline is two years from the date of birth.

<Checklist items={["Childbirth cost subsidy application form", "Receipt or itemized statement showing the delivery cost you paid", "Documentation of your health insurance coverage", "Documentation confirming you received the childbirth lump-sum allowance", "Documentation for supplementary benefits or stillbirth/miscarriage certification, if applicable"]} />

You can submit your application in person or by mail to the Child-Rearing Promotion Division on the 2nd floor of the ward office, or apply online through the Chiyoda Ward portal site.
