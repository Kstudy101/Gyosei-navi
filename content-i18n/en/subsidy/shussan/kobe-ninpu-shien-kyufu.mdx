Kobe City's Pregnancy, Childbirth and Child-Rearing Yorisoi Support Program (妊娠・出産・子育て寄り添い支援事業) uses the national "Support Benefit for Pregnant Women" (妊婦のための支援給付) to provide consultation support from pregnancy through childbirth and child-rearing, together with financial support through the Yorisoi Benefit (寄り添い給付金). The Yorisoi Benefit pays ¥50,000 during pregnancy and ¥50,000 per child at birth (¥100,000 in total for a single baby, and ¥100,000 at birth for twins).

## Key points

- **¥50,000 during pregnancy**, and **¥50,000 per child at birth** (**¥100,000** at birth for twins)
- **No income limit**. For a single baby, receiving both adds up to **¥100,000**
- Apply **online through e-KOBE** after receiving guidance at your pregnancy notification or newborn home visit
- Miscarriage or stillbirth after heartbeat confirmation on or after April 1, 2025 is **also covered** (procedures vary by situation)

## 1. Who qualifies and how payment works

Kobe City provides consultation support and financial support (the Yorisoi Benefit) as a set. Eligible applicants and amounts for the financial support are as follows.

| Type | Who can apply / receive | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Yorisoi Benefit, pregnancy stage | Pregnant woman | ¥50,000 |
| Yorisoi Benefit, birth stage | New mother | ¥50,000 per child (¥100,000 for twins) |

- **There is no income limit**
- People whose child died after birth can still receive both the pregnancy-stage and birth-stage benefits
- People who lost a child through miscarriage or stillbirth (including induced termination) after a medical institution confirmed the fetal heartbeat **on or after April 1, 2025** are eligible for both the pregnancy-stage and birth-stage benefits

The birth-stage application guidance page says eligibility requires that you have had an interview such as a newborn home visit and that you were **registered as a resident of Kobe City at the time of the interview**. For the pregnancy stage, people who filed a pregnancy notification and had an interview are eligible for the application guidance.

## 2. How to apply

You must apply to receive the benefit. After receiving guidance, you complete the procedure online.

### Pregnancy stage

You receive application guidance when you file your pregnancy notification. After receiving it, you apply through e-KOBE for "Yorisoi Benefit / Pregnancy Stage (applications from April 1, 2025 (Reiwa 7))". If you don't have an e-KOBE account, you need to register a new one. Please proceed from the official application guidance page (the official page notes that the form does not appear in e-KOBE's internal search).

The application must be made by the **pregnant woman herself**.

### Birth stage

You receive application guidance at the newborn home visit. After receiving it, you apply through e-KOBE for "Yorisoi Benefit / Birth Stage (births from April 1, 2025 (Reiwa 7))". Here too, if you don't have an account, you register a new one before applying.

The application must be made by the **new mother herself**.

If you will not receive Kobe City's newborn home visit, for example because you give birth in your hometown, please contact the Health and Welfare Division of your ward or branch office **in advance**. Even if you have the newborn home visit in your hometown, the benefit is paid by Kobe City. Confirmation may take some time.

### If you don't have a Maternal and Child Health Handbook

A separate application page is available for people without a Maternal and Child Health Handbook (母子健康手帳). The payment framework (¥50,000 for the pregnancy stage / ¥50,000 per child for the birth stage) is the same. Please check the dedicated official application page for details.

## 3. Consultation support (accompanying-style support)

Along with the benefit, public health nurses and other staff provide consultations at the following times.

- When you file your pregnancy notification
- Around the 8th month of pregnancy (for those who want it)
- Newborn home visit (until the baby is 4 months old)

Around the 6th to 7th month of pregnancy, a questionnaire is sent to the email address you registered when applying for the benefit. If you answer that you have worries or difficulties about the birth, a ward public health nurse or other staff member will consult with you. You can also consult your ward's Health and Welfare Division at any time about pregnancy or child-rearing worries, without waiting for the questionnaire or the newborn home visit.

## 4. Moving in, moving out, and other notes

- **Moving in**: If you already filed a pregnancy notification in your previous municipality, you generally apply there for the "Support Benefit for Pregnant Women". If you were unable to, you will receive guidance at the ward's Health and Welfare Division when you register your move to Kobe City
- **Moving out**: If you have not applied in Kobe City, you apply in your new municipality
- **Returning from overseas**: If you returned before giving birth and filed a pregnancy notification in Kobe City, you are eligible for both the pregnancy-stage and birth-stage benefits. Returning after giving birth is generally not eligible (there is an exception if you were pregnant before moving overseas; please consult your ward office)
- **If you have no resident registration, such as after fleeing domestic violence (DV)**: You may be able to receive the benefit if you meet certain requirements. Please consult the Health and Welfare Division of your ward (the Yorisoi Support Program staff)

The official Kobe City pages reviewed for this article do not state how many days payment takes or how many years you have to apply. Please check the official guidance for the latest procedures.

## Summary

Kobe City's Yorisoi Benefit is a cash benefit based on the national Support Benefit for Pregnant Women: ¥50,000 during pregnancy and ¥50,000 per child at birth. The main flow is to receive guidance and then apply through e-KOBE, with consultation support provided alongside. Other programs (for example, the postpartum care program (産後ケア事業)) have separate applications, so check the official guidance for each type of support you need.
