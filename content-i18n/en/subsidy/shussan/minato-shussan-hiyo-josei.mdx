Minato Ward's childbirth cost subsidy covers the gap when delivery costs exceed the childbirth lump-sum allowance and similar national payments. It pays up to ¥310,000 per birth.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The subsidy covers **up to ¥310,000 per birth** — the full gap between your actual delivery cost and the lump-sum allowance
- If your delivery cost is already covered by the lump-sum allowance, the subsidy is zero
- The application deadline is **one year from the date of birth**

## 1. Who qualifies

Eligibility applies to either parent of the newborn, provided all of these are met:

- They had residency in Minato Ward before and after the birth, and have lived there continuously for at least one year as of the application date
- The newborn has had residency in the ward since birth and lives with the parent
- The mother is enrolled in Japan's public health insurance

Stillbirths or miscarriages at 85 days of pregnancy or later that qualify for the childbirth lump-sum allowance are also eligible. People receiving public assistance are not eligible.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

The subsidy equals your actual delivery cost — or the subsidy calculation cap, whichever is lower — minus the childbirth lump-sum allowance and any supplementary benefits.

| Number of babies | Subsidy calculation cap | Maximum subsidy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ¥810,000 | ¥310,000 |
| 2 (twins) | ¥1,290,000 | ¥290,000 |
| 3 (triplets) | ¥1,770,000 | ¥270,000 |

For example, for a single birth costing ¥900,000, subtracting the ¥500,000 lump-sum allowance would give ¥400,000 — but the subsidy is capped at ¥310,000, leaving you with ¥90,000 out of pocket.

## 3. How to apply

The application deadline is one year from the date of birth.

<Checklist items={["Childbirth cost subsidy application form", "Copy of the hospital-issued receipt and itemized statement for delivery costs", "Documentation of the mother's health insurance", "Documentation proving receipt of the childbirth lump-sum allowance", "Copy of the benefit decision notice for any supplementary benefits, if applicable"]} />

You can apply in person at the Health and Welfare Section of your local branch office, by mail, or online through Mynaportal.
