Shinagawa Ward's Pregnancy Support Benefit program, based on the national framework, pays cash to people who complete an interview after filing a pregnancy notification and again after registering the birth. It provides ¥50,000 during pregnancy and another ¥50,000 per child after birth.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The Pregnancy Support Benefit pays **¥50,000 during pregnancy, plus ¥50,000 per child after birth**
- Based on the official page reviewed for this article, there's **no ward-run subsidy tied to actual delivery costs**, unlike Minato or Chiyoda wards
- The Tokyo Metropolitan Government's Akachan First program (worth ¥100,000) requires a separate application

## 1. Who qualifies

| Payment | Who qualifies |
|---|---|
| Pregnancy Support Benefit (pregnancy stage) | Pregnant women who filed a pregnancy notification and support benefit application, and completed an interview with a midwife or public health nurse |
| Pregnancy Support Benefit (post-birth) | New mothers who gave birth, received a Sukusuku Baby home visit, and reported the number of children |

People who experienced a miscarriage, induced termination, or stillbirth, and those whose child died after birth, are also eligible.

## 2. Amount

¥50,000 during pregnancy and ¥50,000 per child after birth, for a maximum of ¥100,000. Separately, the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's Akachan First program (worth ¥100,000) requires its own application.

## 3. How to apply

<Checklist items={["Apply for the Pregnancy Support Benefit (pregnancy stage) using the QR code in the guidance from your pregnancy consultation (first interview)", "Apply for the Pregnancy Support Benefit (post-birth) using the QR code in the guidance from your Sukusuku Baby home visit", "Apply separately for the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's Akachan First program (worth ¥100,000)"]} />

Payments are made to a bank account in the name of the pregnant woman or new mother, and typically take two to three months from application.
