The childbirth lump-sum allowance (出産育児一時金) pays anyone enrolled in Japan's public health insurance ¥500,000 per child when they give birth, funded by their insurer. In April 2023 the standard amount rose from ¥420,000 to ¥500,000 — the first increase in 13 years.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The standard payment is **¥500,000 per child**
- With the "direct payment system," you only pay the hospital the difference between the total cost and the allowance — not the full amount up front
- You have **two years from the day after the birth** to apply

## 1. Who qualifies

You qualify if both of these apply:

- You were enrolled in Japan's public health insurance at the time of birth
- The birth occurred at 4 months (85 days) of pregnancy or later

If you meet both conditions, the method and location of delivery don't affect eligibility. If you give birth within six months of leaving a job, you can choose to claim the allowance from your former insurer instead (as long as you were continuously enrolled with them for at least a year). If you give birth overseas, you can still apply as long as you held valid coverage at the time.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

The standard payment is ¥500,000 per child. If your total delivery costs come in under that amount, you can claim the difference back from your health insurer.

## 3. How payment and applications work

There are three ways the allowance can be paid out and claimed.

| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| Direct payment system | The hospital applies to your insurer on your behalf, and the insurer pays the hospital directly. You avoid paying the full amount up front |
| Receipt-by-proxy system | You apply to your insurer yourself, and the hospital receives the payment on your behalf |
| Reimbursement system | You apply to your insurer yourself and receive the payment yourself (you need to pay the full delivery cost up front at the hospital) |

<Checklist items={["Confirm with your hospital whether it offers the direct payment system (this varies by facility)", "Sign the agreement with the hospital to use the direct payment system", "After the birth, check the itemized delivery cost statement issued by the hospital", "If the total cost is less than the allowance, ask your insurer how to claim the difference"]} />

The exact documents and procedures depend on which insurer you're enrolled with. If you have employee health insurance, check with the Japan Health Insurance Association (協会けんぽ) or your health insurance union. If you're on National Health Insurance (国民健康保険), check with your municipal NHI counter.
