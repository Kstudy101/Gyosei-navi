Itabashi Ward's Pregnancy Support Benefit (妊婦のための支援給付) is a cash support program paid in **two installments**: after your Pregnancy Support Benefit certification at the time of the pregnancy notification, and after you notify the ward of the number of fetuses, which you can do from 8 weeks before your due date. It is offered together with the ward's companion-style pregnancy interview, childcare package, and Sukusuku Card.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key Points

- **¥50,000 at pregnancy + ¥50,000 per child at birth** (¥100,000 in total for a single baby)
- Apply or notify using the **guidance given after your pregnancy interview** (first payment) and the **guidance enclosed with the 8-month pregnancy survey** (second payment)
- Paid **only to an account in the pregnant person's own name**, generally **about 2-3 months** after your application is received

## 1. Who Is Eligible and Conditions

The main conditions confirmed on the official page are as follows.

### First payment (Pregnancy Support Benefit certification)

- You were **pregnant as of April 1, 2025 (Reiwa 7)**, or **became pregnant on or after that date**
- The **fetal heartbeat has been confirmed** at a medical institution
- You are **registered as a resident of Itabashi Ward** on the application date

### Second payment (notification of the number of fetuses)

- You are **due to give birth on or after April 1, 2025 (Reiwa 7)**, or you **have given birth**
- You are **registered as a resident of Itabashi Ward at the time of the notification** of the number of fetuses, and you have received **Itabashi Ward's Pregnancy Support Benefit certification**

You are not eligible if you received cash, gifts, etc. under the "Childbirth and Child-Rearing Support Benefit" (former program) for the same pregnancy, or received benefits under the same program from another municipality (you cannot receive duplicate payments from multiple municipalities, for either the first or second payment).

If you received only the first payment from another municipality and want to receive only the second payment in Itabashi Ward, you must apply again, including a Pregnancy Benefit certification application. For guidance for people moving in, see the official page "For those who have moved into Itabashi Ward".

## 2. Amount and Payment

| Payment | Timing | Amount | Trigger for applying or notifying |
|---|---|---|---|
| First | After Pregnancy Support Benefit certification | ¥50,000 per pregnant person | Guidance given after the pregnancy interview |
| Second | After notification of the number of fetuses | ¥50,000 per child | Guidance sent together with the 8-month pregnancy survey |

The benefit is paid only to a bank account **in the name of the pregnant person herself**. Payment is generally made **about 2-3 months** after your application (notification) is received. If there are errors or missing items, it may take longer.

## 3. Application Deadlines

| Payment | Application / notification deadline |
|---|---|
| First | 2 years from the date the fetal heartbeat was confirmed at a medical institution |
| Second | 2 years from the date 8 weeks before the due date |

## 4. How to Apply

### Moving in or out

- **Moving in during pregnancy**: After you submit a pregnant person's move-in notification to the Health and Welfare Center, you will receive the relevant guidance when you have your pregnancy interview.
- **Moving in after giving birth**: Call the Tsunagaru ITABASHI Call Center to be told whether the former program or the Pregnancy Support Benefit applies to you.
- **Moving out**: If you are registered as a resident of Itabashi Ward at the time of certification (receipt of your notification), the ward will make the payment even after you move out. If you moved out before certification, contact your new municipality.

### Miscarriage or stillbirth

If you have a miscarriage, stillbirth, or termination of pregnancy after submitting your pregnancy notification, the ward states that the application guidance and payment process are the same as above. You may also be eligible if the miscarriage occurred before the pregnancy notification, so if you wish to apply, please contact the Health Promotion Division, Maternal and Child Health Section (03-3579-2313) or the call center (0120-100-619).

## 5. Other Ward Services Worth Knowing

Separately from the cash benefit, people who have had a pregnancy interview receive the **Itabashi Ward Childcare Package (e-gift)**. It cannot be reissued, and the ward advises saving your data before changing phones (the page does not give details on the amount or items).

There is also the **Sukusuku Card** distributed when you submit your pregnancy notification (or a pregnant person's move-in notification): 6 coupons per child, valid from pregnancy until the day before the child's 3rd birthday. If you receive a Sukusuku Card on or after April 1, 2026 (Reiwa 8), you are eligible for **Sukusuku Points** (2,000 points on Itabashi Pay), for which you can apply from October 1, 2026 (Reiwa 8).

Within the Itabashi Ward pregnancy and childbirth pages we checked this time, we found no ward-original actual-cost subsidy based on your out-of-pocket childbirth costs, such as those in Minato Ward or Chiyoda Ward. Since a program may exist on another page, we do not state that there is none.
