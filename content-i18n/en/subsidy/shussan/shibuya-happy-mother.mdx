Shibuya Ward's Happy Mother Childbirth Grant is a ward-run grant for anyone registered as a Shibuya resident who gives birth. It pays up to ¥100,000 per birth.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The grant pays **up to ¥100,000 per birth**
- If your health insurance provides a supplementary benefit, that amount is **subtracted** from the grant
- The application deadline is **one year from the date of birth**

## 1. Who qualifies

You qualify if all of the following apply:

- You gave birth after 12 weeks (85 days) of pregnancy (this also covers miscarriages or stillbirths past 12 weeks)
- You've been continuously registered as a Shibuya resident from three months before the birth through the application date
- You're enrolled in health insurance

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

The grant is capped at ¥100,000 per birth. If your health insurance pays a "supplementary benefit" on top of the childbirth lump-sum allowance, that amount is subtracted from the grant. The grant counts as taxable income (occasional income) for income tax purposes.

## 3. How to apply

The application deadline is one year from the date of birth. You can apply in person, by mail, or through Shibuya Ward's official LINE account.
