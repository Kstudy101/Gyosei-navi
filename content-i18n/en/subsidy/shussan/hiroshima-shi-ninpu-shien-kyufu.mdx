Hiroshima City's Pregnancy Support Benefit (妊婦支援給付金, also called 妊婦のための支援給付) is a cash benefit paid by the city to ease the physical, mental, and financial burden around pregnancy and childbirth. The first payment is ¥50,000 per pregnant person, and the second is ¥50,000 per baby. Both are transferred to an account in the pregnant person's own name.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- First payment: **¥50,000 (cash) per pregnant person** (after pregnancy is confirmed)
- Second payment: **¥50,000 (cash) per baby** (from the day 8 weeks before the expected due date)
- Payment usually arrives **2 to 3 months** after you apply. The account **must be in the pregnant person's own name**
- The application deadline for both payments is **2 years** from each starting date

## 1. Who is eligible and conditions

You are eligible if you meet all of the following.

- You are pregnant and registered as a resident of Hiroshima City
- You became pregnant **on or after April 1, 2025 (Reiwa 7)**
- Your baby's **heartbeat** has been confirmed at a medical institution
- You have **not received the full** Pregnancy Support Benefit from another municipality

If the pregnancy did not continue because of a miscarriage, stillbirth, or similar event after the baby's heartbeat was confirmed on or after April 1, 2025 (Reiwa 7), you are still eligible.

### Transitional rules

If you became pregnant on or before March 31, 2025 (Reiwa 7) and gave birth on or after April 1, 2025 (Reiwa 7), part or all of this benefit may be paid to you. If you have already received the **Childbirth Support Benefit (出産応援給付金)**, this benefit pays **only the number of babies × ¥50,000**.

If the eligible pregnant person has passed away and an heir will receive the payment, please contact the responsible office.

## 2. Amount and how it is calculated

| Payment | Amount | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| First | ¥50,000 (cash) per pregnant person | After pregnancy is confirmed |
| Second | ¥50,000 (cash) per baby (each child you were pregnant with) | From the day 8 weeks before the expected due date |

For a single baby, receiving both payments comes to ¥100,000 in total. The account **must be the pregnant person's own**.

The official Hiroshima City page (the scope reviewed for this article) does not mention any city-specific top-up beyond the national ¥50,000 + ¥50,000. For multiple births, the wording for the first payment is only "per pregnant person," so please confirm in the official guidance whether an increase applies.

## 3. How to apply and required documents

### First payment

When you file a pregnancy notification or complete move-in procedures at the health center in your ward, you will be given a flyer about applying for pregnancy benefit certification. Apply using the QR code on the flyer. After you apply, the money is transferred to the designated account in the pregnant person's name in 2 to 3 months.

### Second payment

A notification guide is mailed to you roughly the month after you file the birth registration. If it does not arrive, contact the health center in your ward. People who have moved in are told about it during move-in procedures at the health center. Submit your notification using the QR code in the guide. After you apply, the money is transferred to the designated account in the pregnant person's name in 2 to 3 months.

### If the pregnancy did not continue

If a medical institution confirmed the baby's heartbeat on or after April 1, 2025 (Reiwa 7), and you then had a miscarriage or similar event without having filed a pregnancy notification with Hiroshima City, a medical certificate is required. The "Medical Certificate for Pregnancy Benefit Certification (妊婦給付認定用診断書)" published by Hiroshima City is needed only when no pregnancy notification was filed. For details, ask the dedicated inquiry line.

### Application deadline

| Payment | Starting date | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| First | The day the baby's heartbeat was confirmed | 2 years from the starting date |
| Second | The day 8 weeks before the expected due date | 2 years from the starting date |

### Contacts

- Applications and payments: Hiroshima City Pregnancy Support Benefit dedicated inquiry line, 082-504-2957 (8:30 to 17:15, closed weekends and holidays)
- Consultations on pregnancy, childbirth, and child-rearing: each ward's Community Support Division (e.g., Naka Ward 082-504-2109)
- Responsible office: Maternal and Child Health Section, Child and Youth Support Division, Children's Future Bureau, 082-504-2623
