Saitama City's "Support Benefit for Pregnant Women" (妊婦のための支援給付) is a national program that started on April 1, 2025 under the Act on Support for Children and Child-Rearing, and the city pays the "Pregnancy Support Benefit" (妊婦支援給付金) in two installments: at pregnancy and after birth. You can choose to receive it as cash (bank transfer) or as digital local currency, and choosing digital earns extra points from the city.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- **First payment ¥50,000 + second payment (¥50,000 per baby)** (¥100,000 in total for a single baby)
- Receive it as **cash** or **digital local currency** (the Saitama City Minna app)
- Digital applications by February 28, 2027 get a **Tamapon and other bonuses** (amounts may change)
- Apply using the QR code on the **Electronic Application Guide** given at your interview (you cannot apply directly from the guide page)

## 1. Who qualifies

### Pregnancy Support Benefit (first payment)

You must meet both of the following.

1. A pregnant woman with **resident registration in Saitama City** at the time of application (the time you apply for pregnancy benefit certification)
2. A woman whose **fetal heartbeat has been confirmed** by a doctor or similar at an obstetric medical institution (the pregnancy has been medically confirmed)

The city's Q&A (dated April 1, 2026) states the heartbeat confirmation as "on or after April 1, 2025." Please check this against the latest guidance.

### Pregnancy Support Benefit (second payment)

You must meet both of the following.

1. A woman who **gave birth on or after April 1, 2025** and has resident registration in the city at the time of application (the time you report the number of babies)
2. A woman who was resident-registered in Japan for some period during pregnancy

### Miscarriage, stillbirth, induced abortion, and loss of a baby after birth

- If you had a miscarriage, stillbirth, or induced abortion on or after April 1, 2025, after a doctor confirmed the fetal heartbeat, you are eligible
- Even before submitting a pregnancy notification, if it is after heartbeat confirmation you are reported to be eligible without an interview (a medical certificate or similar is required; for stillbirth, a copy of the stillbirth notification or stillbirth certificate is also acceptable)
- If your baby passed away after birth, you are also reported to be eligible for the second payment

### Moving in or out

- If you move out of Saitama City before applying, the benefit is paid by **the city or ward you moved to**
- If you moved to Saitama City without receiving the second payment from your previous city, you are eligible for the second payment here
- The applicant and recipient must be **the pregnant woman (or the woman who gave birth) herself**

The official guidance and Q&A do not mention any income limit. If you are unsure, please ask the Maternal and Child Health Division.

## 2. Amount and how it is calculated

| Category | Amount |
|---|---|
| First payment (at pregnancy) | ¥50,000 |
| Second payment (after birth) | ¥50,000 per baby |

Example for twins (official guidance): ¥50,000 for the first payment, ¥50,000 x 2 for the second payment.

### Bonus for choosing digital local currency (city original)

If you choose digital local currency as your payment method, "Childbirth and Child-Raising Support Points" (1 point = ¥1) equal to the benefit are issued. In addition, the following bonuses apply to applications received **through February 28, 2027**.

| Bonus | Details |
|---|---|
| Tamapon | **¥5,000 worth** for both the first and second payments (per baby for the second payment). The amount may change |
| Child-raising household support campaign (second payment only) | In addition to Tamapon, **¥10,000 worth** of Childbirth and Child-Raising Support Points per baby |

- Validity of Childbirth and Child-Raising Support Points: 1 year from the date of issue
- Validity of Tamapon: 180 days from the last issue date or last use date
- Once your application is complete, you cannot change the payment method, and digital local currency cannot be exchanged for cash

## 3. How to apply and required documents

An "Electronic Application Guide" is handed out at the accompanying-support (伴走型相談支援) interview.

- **First payment**: the interview with a maternal and child health counselor (midwife, etc.) when you submit your pregnancy notification
- **Second payment**: the newborn visit or Hello Angel visit, etc., held around 4 months after birth

Apply using the dedicated QR code and the electronic application number (or classification code) on the guide. You cannot apply from the official guide page itself.

### Application deadlines (guideline)

- **First payment**: until the day 2 years have passed from the date the fetal heartbeat was confirmed at the medical institution
- **Second payment**: until the day 2 years have passed from 8 weeks before the due date (if you give birth before the due date, the birth date is the starting point)

### When you are paid

The guideline is the last day of the month after the month the city accepts your online application, or after the month your paper application reaches the city (if there are no problems with it). If the last day of the month is a holiday, it is the preceding weekday; only in December, payment is on the 28th.
