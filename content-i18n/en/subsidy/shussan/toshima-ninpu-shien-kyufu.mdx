Toshima Ward's Pregnancy Support Benefit (妊婦のための支援給付金) is a program under the Act on Child and Child-Rearing Support, paid in two installments: during pregnancy and after birth. You receive ¥50,000 during pregnancy and ¥50,000 per child (fetus) after birth, and you can choose cash or a Tokyo Metropolitan Government gift card.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- **¥50,000 during pregnancy + after birth (number of children x ¥50,000)** (the equivalent of ¥100,000 in total for a single baby)
- You can choose **cash or a Tokyo Metropolitan Government gift**. Cash is transferred **only to an account in the name of the pregnant woman or new mother**
- Ward-original support includes the **Yurikago Support Gift (about ¥10,000)**, the **Toshima Birth Support Coupon (about ¥50,000)**, and the **1st Birthday Support (¥60,000 to ¥80,000)**

## 1. Who qualifies and conditions

The program has been in operation since April 1, 2025. The main conditions confirmed on the official page are as follows.

**1st payment (during pregnancy)**

- Pregnant women who filed a pregnancy notification and applied for pregnancy benefit certification on or after April 1, 2025, and were certified
- Or people who filed a pregnancy notification by March 31, 2025 and did not apply for the Childbirth Support Gift under the former Childbirth and Child-Rearing Support Project
- In either case, you must be **registered as a resident of Toshima Ward at the time of application**
- A doctor's **confirmation of the fetal heartbeat is required** (cases without confirmation are not covered)

**2nd payment (after birth)**

- Women who gave birth on or after April 1, 2025
- You must have a resident record in Toshima Ward **on the date you reported the number of fetuses** and at the time of application

People who have experienced a miscarriage, stillbirth, or induced termination, and those who have lost a child, are also eligible. The fact of the pregnancy and the number of fetuses are confirmed with the Maternal and Child Health Handbook. Even if the miscarriage happened before the pregnancy notification or the Yurikago interview, you can apply with a doctor's certificate or similar document showing that the fetal heartbeat was confirmed. The ward advises contacting the responsible office before applying.

**Ectopic pregnancy is not covered.**

The Toshima Ward page reviewed this time did not state whether there is an income limit.

## 2. Amount and payment method

| Payment | Timing | Amount | How to start the application |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | During pregnancy | ¥50,000 | Online application via the QR code given at the Yurikago interview |
| 2nd | After birth | Number of children (fetuses) x ¥50,000 | Online application via the QR code given at the Konnichiwa Akachan home visit |

You receive the payment in one of the following ways.

- **Cash**: transfer to a bank account in the name of the pregnant woman or new mother (an account in another person's name cannot be specified)
- **Gift**: a Tokyo Metropolitan Government gift card that can be used for childbirth and childcare items, home appliances, and more

The overview page of financial support also says the 2nd payment is made according to the number of children in the case of multiple births.

## 3. How to apply

<Checklist items={["Submit your pregnancy notification and receive your Maternal and Child Health Handbook", "Attend the Yurikago interview and apply online for the 1st payment (¥50,000) using the QR code you are given", "After birth, receive the Konnichiwa Akachan home visit and apply online for the 2nd payment (number of children x ¥50,000) using the QR code you are given", "If you choose cash, specify an account in your own name; if you choose the gift, receive it as instructed"]} />

The Yurikago interview is by appointment (priority given to those with reservations) and is held at the Toshima City Public Health Center (Health Promotion Division) or the Nagasaki Health Consultation Center. It is available to pregnant women who are registered as residents of the ward on the day of the interview.

For inquiries, contact the Health Promotion Division Administration and Projects Group (phone 03-3987-4173) or the Nagasaki Health Consultation Center Administration and Projects Group (phone 03-3957-1191).

## 4. Other ward-original financial support to know about

Apart from the national Pregnancy Support Benefit, Toshima Ward's financial support overview lists the following.

| Support | Details | Main conditions and how to receive |
|---|---|---|
| Yurikago Support Gift | Electronic coupon worth **about ¥10,000** | When you use the Yurikago interview |
| Toshima Birth Support Coupon | Electronic coupon worth **about ¥50,000** | Households living in the ward and raising a child born on or after April 1, 2024. **Kosodate Yell registration** is required. Once per child until the child turns 1 (per child in the case of multiple births) |
| Kosodate Yell post-visit coupon | **About ¥3,000** per visit (up to 8 times) | People who received a monitoring visit when the child was 4 to 11 months old |
| 1st Birthday Support (FY2026) | 1st child **¥60,000** / 2nd child **¥70,000** / 3rd child and later **¥80,000** (web catalog gift points) | Children living in the ward who turn 1 between April 1, 2026 and March 31, 2027, and their guardians. A questionnaire is required (a visit or the web, depending on daycare attendance) |

For inquiries about the Toshima Birth Support Coupon, contact the Child-Rearing Household Monitoring Visit Program Call Center (0570-033100) or the Child-Rearing Support Division Support Group (03-4566-2478). For the Birthday Support, contact the Child and Family Support Center Policy Coordination Group (03-6858-7822) and the program office (0120-187-533).
