Kumamoto City's "Welcome Baby Project" (ようこそ赤ちゃんプロジェクト) is the city's version of the national Pregnancy Support Benefit (妊婦のための支援給付). It combines financial support, the "Welcome Baby Gift" (ようこそ赤ちゃんギフト), with companion-style counseling support. It started on April 1, 2025 (Reiwa 7), and pays ¥50,000 in cash per pregnant person at the pregnancy notification and ¥50,000 in cash per child after the birth is registered (¥100,000 in total for a single birth).

## Key points

- **¥50,000 at the pregnancy notification** (per pregnant person)
- **¥50,000 per child after the birth is registered** (¥100,000 in total for a single birth; ¥150,000 in total for twins)
- **No income limit**. Covers pregnancies on or after April 1, 2025 (Reiwa 7)
- After you receive guidance at the interview or the Konnichiwa Akachan home visit, you **apply online**. The transfer appears under the name 「クマモトアカチヤンギフト」

## 1. Who qualifies and payment conditions

The Welcome Baby Gift covers the following people.

- **People who became pregnant on or after April 1, 2025 (Reiwa 7)**
- **There is no income limit**
- The official page states that everyone who becomes pregnant from now on will be eligible
- **People who had a miscarriage or stillbirth on or after April 1, 2025 (Reiwa 7)** are also eligible (contact the city for details)

The official page states that the post-birth payment also applies in cases of miscarriage, stillbirth and similar. For your individual circumstances, please check with Kumamoto City (096-328-2158).

## 2. Amount and how it is calculated

| Category | Who it is paid for | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| At pregnancy notification | Pregnant person | ¥50,000 per pregnant person |
| After birth registration | Child | ¥50,000 per child |

- For a single birth, receiving both comes to **¥100,000 in total**
- In the official example, twins receive ¥50,000 at the pregnancy notification and ¥100,000 after the birth, for **¥150,000 in total**
- Payment is made in cash. If there are no problems in the review, it is paid in roughly **2 months**
- The transfer appears under the name 「クマモトアカチヤンギフト」
- A **notice** of the review result is mailed to eligible applicants

## 3. How to apply and deadlines

You must apply to receive the benefit. You first receive guidance, then apply online.

### At the pregnancy notification

After your interview with a staff member at the time of the pregnancy notification, you are handed the application guidance directly. Please apply online following the notice. The application deadline is **within 2 years** of the date your pregnancy was confirmed at a medical institution (the date a doctor confirmed the fetal heartbeat).

### After the birth is registered

During the home visit under the Konnichiwa Akachan program, you are handed the application guidance directly. You again apply online. The application deadline is from **8 weeks before the due date until 2 years after**.

The specific URL of the online application portal is expected to be given in the official guidance notice and is not shown on the program page itself. If you lose the guidance, please check with the contact office.

## 4. Relationship to companion-style counseling support

Alongside the financial support, public health nurses in charge of each school district at the Child and Family Center (こども家庭センター, the Health and Children's Division of each ward office) provide counseling support at the pregnancy notification interview, the Konnichiwa Akachan home visit and other occasions. The guidance for the gift is also given at these interviews and visits. For childcare consultations, please contact the Health and Children's Division of the ward where you live.

## 5. Points to note

- A doctor's certificate is generally not required (your pregnancy notification, Maternal and Child Health Handbook and similar documents are used to confirm). There are exceptions, such as when a miscarriage occurred without a pregnancy notification being filed, or when a multiple pregnancy must be confirmed
- The page clearly says "pregnancy on or after April 1, 2025 (Reiwa 7)". Whether pregnancies before March 31, 2025 have any transitional coverage cannot be confirmed from this page
- The page does not explain in detail how moving into or out of the city is handled, so please check with the city if this applies to you
