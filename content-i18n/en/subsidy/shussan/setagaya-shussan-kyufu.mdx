Giving birth in Setagaya Ward means you can receive two separate cash benefits: the national "Pregnancy Support Benefit" and the ward's own "Setagaya Childbirth Cost Grant." Together they add up to ¥150,000 for a single birth.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The Pregnancy Support Benefit pays **¥50,000 for the first payment, plus ¥50,000 per child for the second**
- The Childbirth Cost Grant pays a **flat ¥50,000 per child**, regardless of actual delivery cost
- This differs from Minato and Chiyoda wards, which reimburse actual delivery costs up to ¥310,000

## 1. Pregnancy Support Benefit (national program)

Since April 1, 2025, this benefit has been available to all pregnant women and child-rearing households.

| Payment | Who qualifies | Amount | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| First payment | Pregnant women registered as Setagaya residents at the time of application | ¥50,000 per pregnant woman | Two years from the date pregnancy is confirmed by a medical institution |
| Second payment | Same as above | ¥50,000 × number of children | Two years from eight weeks before the due date |

You'll receive application guidance for the first payment at your Neubora consultation (a pregnancy-stage interview) and for the second payment during the newborn home visit. Both are applied for online.

## 2. Setagaya Childbirth Cost Grant (ward-run program)

This is a flat **¥50,000** grant per newborn, funded by the ward. It's available to newborns with a Setagaya address at the time of birth (or, in the case of a miscarriage or stillbirth at 85 days of pregnancy or later, to the mother). The application deadline is one year from the date of birth, and the grant counts as taxable income (occasional income) for income tax purposes.
