The subsidies available to pet owners in Japan are not a nationwide benefit paid directly by the national government. Most are "cat spay/neuter cost assistance" (猫の不妊去勢手術費助成) run by individual cities and wards. In the scope we checked, we found no programs for dogs and no national subsidy for the cost of microchipping itself.

## Summary

- The most common "pet subsidy" you will find is **cat spay/neuter cost assistance** (programs vary by municipality)
- Assistance for dog spay/neuter surgery was **not found** in the municipalities we surveyed
- **No direct national (Ministry of the Environment) subsidy for the cost of microchipping itself was found.** This is separate from the mandatory implantation and registration rules
- Municipalities differ on whether they cover "owned cats" or "ownerless cats (stray cats and community cats)" — always check which animals are eligible before applying

## 1. Cat spay/neuter cost assistance: programs run by each municipality

There is no nationwide national program that subsidizes spay/neuter surgery for dogs and cats. We surveyed several Tokyo wards (Chiyoda, Minato, Shinjuku, and Shibuya) and confirmed that each runs its own **assistance program for cats**.

The programs fall into two main types.

| Type | Eligible animals | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| For ownerless cats | Cats living in the neighborhood (owned cats are not eligible) | Community cat activities and controlling breeding | Chiyoda, Minato |
| For owned cats | Cats kept in a home | Promoting proper pet care | Shibuya |
| Both | Both owned and ownerless cats (as separate programs) | Covers both | Shinjuku |

The amount varies by municipality, ranging from a few thousand yen to tens of thousands of yen. Specific amounts and application requirements are explained in each municipality's own article.

## 2. About assistance for dog spay/neuter surgery

As far as we could confirm on the official websites of the four wards surveyed (Chiyoda, Minato, Shinjuku, and Shibuya), **no assistance program for dog spay/neuter surgery was found**. Programs for cats have spread in connection with administrative issues such as neighborhood problems and breeding control related to "community cats," and it is thought that dogs are rarely allowed to roam freely, so similar issues are less likely to arise (this is an inference, not a reason officially given by the municipalities).

Whether assistance for dog spay/neuter surgery exists must be checked individually with each municipality. The fact that we could not find one here does not mean it exists "nowhere in Japan."

## 3. Microchipping and help with its cost

Since June 1, 2022, microchip implantation and information registration have been legally mandatory for dogs and cats sold by dog and cat sellers (breeders, pet shops, and so on). For dogs and cats kept in ordinary households, implantation is a **best-effort obligation** (not legally enforceable), but registering the information is mandatory if a chip has been implanted.

<Callout type="warning">
The Ministry of the Environment's official pages do not mention any program in which the national government directly subsidizes the cost of microchip implantation. They only guide you to the information registration fee (which is charged) and introduce subsidy programs that some municipalities run on their own. The list of subsidizing municipalities that the Ministry had identified as of June 2023 did not include any municipality in Tokyo.
</Callout>

As with assistance for dog spay/neuter surgery, whether there is help with microchipping costs must be checked individually with your municipality.
