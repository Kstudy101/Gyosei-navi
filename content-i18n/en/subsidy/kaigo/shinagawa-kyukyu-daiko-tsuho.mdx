Shinagawa Ward's elderly emergency response system is a free monitoring and emergency notification service the ward provides for residents 65 and older who live alone or in elderly-only households. You can apply regardless of long-term care certification.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- It's for residents **65 or older living alone**, or **in an elderly-only household** (long-term care certification isn't required)
- Usage is **free**
- If an emergency pendant or sensor is triggered, it **notifies a security company**, which dispatches help

## 1. Who qualifies

You qualify if you have an address in the ward and fall into one of these categories:

- Living alone and 65 or older
- Part of an elderly-only household
- Part of an elderly household that's alone during the day or at night

Long-term care certification isn't required, but the application window differs. If you have a long-term care certification, apply through your home care support center; if you don't, apply through a Sasae-ai Hot Station.

## 2. What the service covers and its cost

There's no monthly usage fee. If a medical emergency or accident happens at home, any of the following triggers a notification to a security company, which dispatches staff to provide prompt assistance and rescue:

- Pressing the notification button on an emergency pendant
- A room-based activity sensor being triggered
- A fire sensor being triggered

## 3. How to apply

<Checklist items={["Consult with your local home care support center, or a Sasae-ai Hot Station", "Prepare the application form and equipment installation checksheet", "You can also apply at the ward office counter or through the electronic application system"]} />

People with disabilities who meet certain requirements may also qualify for the "Shinagawa Ward Disability Emergency Response System."
