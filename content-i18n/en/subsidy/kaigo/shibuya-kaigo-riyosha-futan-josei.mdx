Shibuya Ward's long-term care insurance service co-payment subsidy is a ward-run reduction program that subsidizes 70% of the out-of-pocket cost for eligible services like home-visit care, for people who meet income and asset requirements.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- **70%** of your co-payment for eligible services gets subsidized (some services also get **25%** of out-of-pocket meal and lodging costs)
- It's for people certified as needing **long-term care, support, or as a program-eligible person**, with a 10% co-payment and income/assets at or below the standard thresholds
- You pay the provider **in full first**, then apply to the ward for reimbursement

## 1. Who qualifies

You qualify if you meet all of the following:

<Checklist items={["Certified as needing long-term care, support, or as a program-eligible person", "Your co-payment rate is 10%", "Your household's annual income is at or below the standard threshold", "Your household's savings are at or below the standard threshold", "No outstanding long-term care insurance premium delinquency", "Not a recipient of public assistance under the Public Assistance Act"]} />

Standard income and savings thresholds by household size are as follows:

| Household size | Income threshold A (renting) | Income threshold B (own home, etc.) | Savings threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 person | ¥2,400,000 | ¥2,000,000 | ¥3,500,000 |
| 2 people | ¥3,400,000 | ¥2,750,000 | ¥4,500,000 |
| 3 people | ¥4,400,000 | ¥3,500,000 | ¥5,500,000 |
| 4 people | ¥5,400,000 | ¥4,250,000 | ¥6,500,000 |

## 2. Eligible services and subsidy amount

70% of the co-payment you paid for eligible services is subsidized. A wide range of services qualify, including home-visit care, home-visit bathing care, home-visit nursing, home-visit rehabilitation, day care, day rehabilitation, short-stay daily life care, and short-stay medical care.

For some day-service and short-stay-type services, 25% of your out-of-pocket meal costs (or meal and lodging costs) is subsidized on top of that.

There's also a separate "reduction for people with disabilities" (which waives the home-visit care co-payment) for people who used disability welfare home-help services with zero co-payment during roughly the year before their long-term care certification.

## 3. How to apply

You pay the full co-payment to the provider first, then apply at the Long-Term Care Insurance Division counter on the 5th floor of the ward office, and the ward pays out the subsidy amount afterward.

<Checklist items={["Prepare the Long-Term Care Insurance Service Co-Payment Subsidy Application form", "Fill out the income declaration form", "Prepare copies of all savings passbooks (including securities) for yourself and everyone in your household (showing bank name, branch name, account type, account holder name pages, and a page showing recent transactions)", "Submit at the Long-Term Care Insurance Division counter on the 5th floor of the ward office, or by mail"]} />
