Shinjuku Ward's diaper cost subsidy is a ward-run elderly welfare service that covers part of the cost of diapers for elderly residents needing long-term care who require them on a daily basis.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- The subsidy cap is **¥10,000 a month**, and within that range you pay **about 10%** out of pocket
- There are two types: an **in-kind subsidy** where you choose from a catalog, and a **cost subsidy** for people who are hospitalized
- Starting with usage from **April 2024**, care-need level 1 and Category 2 insured persons were added as eligible, and the subsidy cap was raised from ¥7,000 to ¥10,000 a month

## 1. Who qualifies

You qualify if you're registered as a Shinjuku Ward resident and meet all of the following:

- Certified as needing long-term care level 1 or higher (including Category 2 insured persons), or 65 or older and hospitalized in a medical institution
- Require diapers on a daily basis
- Fall within long-term care insurance premium brackets 1-8
- Not receiving the disability diaper cost subsidy

Residents of special nursing homes, long-term care health facilities, long-term care medical facilities, and similar don't qualify for this subsidy since they're covered under long-term care insurance instead.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| Subsidy cap | ¥10,000 a month (¥7,000 a month for usage through November 2023) |
| Your co-payment | About 10% of your diaper costs, within the subsidy cap |
| Anything over the cap | Entirely out of pocket |
| No co-payment | Households exempt from resident tax, on public assistance, or receiving support payments for repatriated Japanese from China (except for amounts over the cap) |

The subsidy is calculated monthly, and you can't use both the in-kind and cost subsidies in the same month. Your subsidy starts the month your application is received — it can't be applied retroactively to before you applied.

## 3. How to use it (in-kind subsidy and cost subsidy)

<Checklist items={["[In-kind subsidy] Choose your desired product from the catalog and order directly from the vendor (delivered once a month; pay your co-payment on receipt)", "[Cost subsidy] For people for whom in-kind assistance isn't practical, such as those hospitalized at facilities that don't allow bringing in your own diapers (submit copies of receipts four times a year, every three months)", "Apply at the Elderly Support Division (2nd floor, counters 5 and 6, ward office main building) or at any Elderly Comprehensive Consultation Center"]} />

There's also a separate "diaper arrangement program" for residents roughly 65 and older who don't qualify for this subsidy, where a ward-affiliated vendor takes orders and delivers, but the full diaper cost is out of pocket under that program.
