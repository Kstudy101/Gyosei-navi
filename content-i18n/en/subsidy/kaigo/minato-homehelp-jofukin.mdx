Minato Ward's long-term care insurance home-help service co-payment subsidy is a ward-run program that subsidizes part of the co-payment for people meeting income and asset requirements. It's a ward-specific reduction layered on top of the national long-term care insurance system.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- There are two types of subsidy: **(1) 7/10 of your co-payment for eligible services**, or **(2) half of whatever falls between ¥15,000 and ¥24,600 a month (up to ¥4,800/month)**
- It's for people certified as needing long-term care or support whose **entire household is exempt from resident tax** and whose **total savings and securities are ¥5,000,000 or less**, among other requirements
- You pay the full amount to the provider first, and **the ward reimburses you roughly two months later**

## 1. Who qualifies

You qualify if you're certified as needing long-term care or support and meet all of the following:

<Checklist items={["You're not receiving public assistance or similar", "Everyone in your household has filed a resident tax return", "Everyone in your household is exempt from resident tax", "You're not a dependent of someone who pays resident tax", "Your household's total savings and securities are ¥5,000,000 or less", "No one in your household owns a vacation home, condo, or similar property besides your primary residence", "No one in your household has outstanding long-term care insurance premium delinquencies", "You're not subject to a benefit restriction due to long-term care insurance premium delinquency"]} />

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

| Subsidy type | Eligible services | Subsidy |
|---|---|---|
| (1) Long-term care insurance home-help service co-payment subsidy | Home-visit care, home-visit bathing care, home-visit nursing, home-visit rehabilitation, regular/on-demand home-visit care and nursing, night-time home-visit care, visiting-type services, etc. | 7/10 of your co-payment (excluding amounts covered by the high-cost care service benefit) |
| (2) Long-term care insurance co-payment subsidy | All long-term care insurance services (excluding specific welfare equipment purchases, home renovation costs, and facility service residence/meal costs) | Half of whatever falls between ¥15,000 and ¥24,600 a month (up to ¥4,800/month) |

(2) applies to people who meet all the requirements for (1) and whose combined taxable pension income and other income exceeds ¥826,500.

## 3. How to apply

You pay the full co-payment to the provider first, and the ward reimburses you roughly two months later.

<Checklist items={["Prepare the application form (Long-Term Care Insurance Home-Help Service Co-Payment Subsidy Application)", "Fill out the savings declaration form, listing Japan Post Bank and other bank accounts separately", "Prepare copies of all passbooks for every household member (showing bank name, branch name, account number, and account holder name pages, plus a balance page from within the last two months)", "If you hold securities, also prepare documents showing the account balance", "Submit in person (Minato Ward Office main building), by mail, or through the electronic application system (fax is not accepted)"]} />
