Setagaya Ward's family caregiver allowance recognizes families who care for someone with a high care-need level at home for a full year without relying on long-term care insurance services, paying them ¥100,000 a year for their effort.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- It's for families who **don't use long-term care insurance services for a year** while caring at home for someone certified as needing **long-term care level 2 (dementia independence level 2 or higher) or level 3 or above**
- The payment is **¥100,000 a year** (flat amount)
- Both the person cared for and the caregiver must be in a household in **Setagaya Ward exempt from resident tax**

## 1. Who qualifies

Both the person receiving care and the person providing it must meet all requirements.

**Requirements for the person receiving care**

- Certified as needing long-term care level 2 (dementia independence level 2 or higher only) or level 3 or above, with at least a year passed since the certification period began
- Hasn't used long-term care insurance services during that certification period (except for up to 10 days a year of short-stay care, welfare equipment rental, specific welfare equipment purchases, or home renovation benefits), and hasn't been hospitalized for 90 days or more in that year
- Has an address in Setagaya Ward and is in a household exempt from resident tax (the location where care is provided must also be in the ward)

**Requirements for the caregiver**

- A family member (including relatives and same-sex partners) who lives with and cares for the person above
- Has an address in Setagaya Ward and is in a household exempt from resident tax
- Isn't receiving payment or compensation for providing care

## 2. Amount

¥100,000 a year (flat amount).

## 3. How to apply

You must apply within two years of the date you became eligible.
