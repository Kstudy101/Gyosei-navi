Chiyoda Ward's diaper supply program is an elderly welfare service the ward runs on its own, delivering diapers each month to elderly residents certified as needing long-term care level 1 or higher. It's classified as a "ward-specific long-term care service," separate from standard long-term care insurance benefits.

<SubsidyInfoCard />

## Key points

- It's for people certified as needing **long-term care level 1 or higher** (or residents 65+ who are hospitalized and in a similarly dependent condition)
- Purchases up to **¥10,600 (tax included) a month** cost just **10%** out of pocket
- If you're hospitalized or in a facility using diapers designated by the hospital, you switch to a cash subsidy (**up to ¥13,200 a month**)

## 1. Who qualifies

You qualify if you're a Chiyoda Ward resident and meet either of the following:

- You're certified as needing long-term care level 1 or higher and require diapers at all times
- You're 65 or older, hospitalized under medical insurance, and in a similarly dependent condition

Residents certified as needing support only, or as independent, don't qualify.

## 2. Amount and how it's calculated

| Category | Your share |
|---|---|
| Purchases up to ¥10,600 (tax included) a month | 10% of the purchase price |
| Anything over ¥10,600 a month | Entirely out of pocket |
| Households on public assistance | Free for purchases up to ¥10,600 |
| Hospitalized or in a facility using hospital-designated diapers (cash subsidy) | Up to ¥13,200 a month |

## 3. How to use the program

You choose products, combinations, and quantities from a catalog and place your order with a designated vendor. Deliveries arrive at your home or elsewhere between the 5th and 7th of each month.
